Projet : RY-FLOOD PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY RECONSTRUCTION ADDITIONAL FINANCING II
Identifiant : P115178
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 41.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 2009
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Objectives of the Project are to assist the Recipient in: (a) protecting residents, economic activities and infrastructure from destructive effects of seasonal flooding in Taiz, Hadramout and AlMahra; (b) restoring access to critical road infrastructure damaged by the floods; and (c) strenhgthening the capacity of local governments and support decentralization.
