Projet : Adaptive Safety Nets and Economic Inclusion Project
Identifiant : P175363
Pays : Cameroon (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 160.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 73 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to: (i) expand the coverage and shock-responsive capacity of the safety nets system for poor households, and (ii) increase access to income-generation and entrepreneurship support opportunities for youth in urban areas.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Adaptive Safety Nets and Economic Inclusion Project for Cameroon is to expand the coverage and shock‑responsive capacity of the safety nets system for poor households and to increase access to income‑generation and entrepreneurship support opportunities for youth in urban areas. The project comprises four components: (i) Adaptive Safety Nets, which includes Regular Cash Transfers, Emergency Cash Transfers, and the Labor‑Intensive Public Works (LIPW) Program; (ii) Youth Economic Inclusion and Entrepreneurship, comprising Economic Inclusion of Youth and a Business Plan Competition; (iii) Shock‑Responsive Digital Delivery Systems, including the Unified Social Registry and a Digital Payment System; and (iv) Project Management and Coordination Support, which finances technical advisory services and other material assistance required for project implementation.
