Projet : Cash for Nutrition and Livelihoods Project
Identifiant : P514855
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 101.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 36 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To provide targeted cash transfers and create livelihood opportunities to improve nutrition while strengthening delivery systems and institutions.

Résumé :
The proposed operation aims to improve nutrition and household resilience by providing targeted, time-bound cash transfers and linked livelihood support while strengthening delivery systems. The project will target areas where malnutrition and stunting are prevalent, delivering six-month Cash for Nutrition (CfN) benefits to pregnant women and mothers of children under two in covered communities. These transfers will be complemented by Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs) that prioritize C4N beneficiaries, enabling savings, microenterprise, and income-generating activities to sustain nutrition gains beyond the benefit period. Implementation will be sequenced geographically and closely coordinated with the World Bank's proposed Yemen Health, Nutrition and WASH Project , with aims to deliver integrated health, nutrition and WASH services to targeted areas of Yemen while building institutional capacity. The project components include (i) targeted cash transfers and nutrition counseling for pregnant women and mothers with children under two; (ii) livelihood support through (a) VSLA formation, capitalization, and enterprise coaching and (b) a microwork for youth pilot; and (iii) delivery systems strengthening for targeting, payments, monitoring, and multisectoral coordination.
