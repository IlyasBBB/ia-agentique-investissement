Projet : Innovations Towards Learning in Lagging Iraqi Governorates
Identifiant : P175721
Pays : Iraq (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 10.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 18 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To enhance teaching practices of Arabic and Mathematics teachers and improve literacy and numeracy skills among the most vulnerable primary students in lagging Iraqi governorates.

Résumé :
The project supports the most vulnerable primary students in acquiring early literacy and numeracy skills in three lagging Iraqi governorates, through support to schools, teachers, caregivers, as well as Directorates of Education (DOE) and the Ministry of Education (MOED). The project’s component 1 aims to strengthen teaching practices of Arabic and Mathematics primary teachers in literacy and numeracy. Component 2 aims to support parents and schools with tailored learning tools to serve students while they learn at home or at school. Component 3 aims to support capacity building for school leaders, DOE and MOED staff, Monitoring and Evaluation, and the Project Management Team (PMT).
