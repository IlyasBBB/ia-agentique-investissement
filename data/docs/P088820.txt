Projet : Poverty Reduction Support Operation (PRSO)
Identifiant : P088820
Pays : Georgia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 20.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 10 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
This operation supports Georgia's PRSP (or EDPRP as it is known in Georgia) by (i) strengthening public sector accountability, efficiency and transparency; (ii) improving electricity and gas sector services; (iii) improving the environment for private sector development; and (iv) improving social protection, education and health care services.

Résumé :
The Poverty Reduction Support Operation (PRSO) Project for Georgia, the first in a series of three proposed PRSOs, is intended to support the Government of Georgia with the implementation o f reforms and policy priorities of Economic Development and Poverty Reduction Program (EDPRP, or the PRSP as it i s known in Georgia). The PRSO will support the objectives and the policies of the EDPRP by: a) strengthening public sector accountability, efficiency, and transparency; b) improving electricity and gas sector services; c) improving the environment for private sector development; and d) improving social protection, education and health care services. The success of this policy operation in Georgia depends basically on two main factors. First, the operation needs to well defined and focused on priority issues where the medium term strategy is well understood and accepted by the government. Second, any reform program must be grounded in a realistic assessment of the government's implementation capacity.
