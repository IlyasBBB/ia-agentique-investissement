Projet : National Environmental Policy Reform Project
Identifiant : P035733
Pays : Dominican Republic (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 3.62 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 56 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE OBJECTIVES ARE TO IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF LIFE AND REDUCE PUBLICHEALTH RISKS THRU EXPANDING WATER, SEWERAGE AND SANITATION SERVICESTO THEIR COMMUNITIES; TO ESTABLISH AN EFFECTIVE POLICY AND REGULATORYFRAMEWORK FOR COASTAL ZONE DEVELOPMENT.

Résumé :
The National Environmental Policy Reform Project's objective is to establish the basis for improved environmental management by defining environmental policy reforms and elaborating a National Environmental Management Program (NEMP) through a learning and participatory process. There are three components in this project. First, supporting policy and institutional reform will define priority legal, institutional, and fiscal reforms to improve environmental management, define sectoral action plans for environmental management reform, and develop a NEMP. Second, developing pilot activities will develop innovative models for natural resource management, and increase decentralization of environmental functions. The third component, aiming at building capacity for project management and providing technical training, will strengthen capacity for environmental planning and management in key environmental agencies; ensure efficient and effective project implementation; and develop the needed human resources for the new institutional arrangements resulting from the reforms.
