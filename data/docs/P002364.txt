Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THIS ADJUSTMENT OPERATION SUPPORTS A POLICY REFORM PROGRAM GEARED TOWA RDS ENCOURAGING THE PROVATE SECTOR SUPPLY RESPONSE TO THE DEVALUATION OF THE CFAF. THE PROGRAM INCLUDES: (I) LIBERALIZATION OF DOMESTIC AN D EXTERNAL TRADE; (II) IMPROVEMENT OF EXPORT AND INVESTMENT INCENTIVE SYSTEMS; (III) REDUCTION OF MARITIME TRANSPORT COSTS; AND (IV) ELIMINA TION OF RIGIDITIES IN THE LABOR MARKET.
