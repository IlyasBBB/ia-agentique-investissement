Projet : First Additional Financing for the Lisungi Emergency COVID-19 Response Project
Identifiant : P177453
Pays : Congo, Republic of (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 83.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To provide emergency income support to households affected by the COVID-19 health and economic crisis and to increase access to productive safety nets for poor and vulnerable households and youth in participating areas in the territory of the Recipient.

Résumé :
The proposed Additional Financing (AF) aims to scale up existing activities under the project and to expand its scope to support the economic inclusion of youth. The AF will expand access to social safety nets (SSN), improve access to income-earning opportunities for vulnerable youth and women, and strengthen the social protection system.
