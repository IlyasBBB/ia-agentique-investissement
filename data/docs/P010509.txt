Projet : MULTIMODAL TRANSIT
Identifiant : P010509
Pays : Nepal (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 28.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 70 mois
Année d'approbation : 1997
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT WILL HELP PROVIDE MORE EFFICIENT FACILITIES & INSTITUTIONSFOR NEPAL'S FOREIGN TRANSIT TRADE AND BILATERAL TRADE THROUGH INDIA.THE PROJECT WILL PROVIDE A 4-KM RAILWAY SIDING FROM INDIA'S BORDER,INLAND FREIGHT TERMINALS AT BIRGUNJ AND BIRATNAGAR IN NEPAL, AND TAFOR TRANSIT TRADE MANAGEMENT, TRADE FACILITATION, AND TRAINING.

Résumé :
The principal development objective of the Multimodal Transit and Trade Facilitation Project in Nepal is to reduce transport costs associated with Nepal's imports and exports. A second set of project objectives are to streamline trade and transit procedures and to improve the efficiency and organization of transit trade documentation and data exchange. There are nine components: (1) The construction of an inland container depot (ICD) at Sirsiya (Birgunj) comprises civil works for a modern multimodal transfer terminal and actions related to implementing the ACRP (Acquisition, Compensation, and Rehabilitation Plan). (2) The next component improves the road ICD at Biratnagar. (3) The civil works required to improve the road-only facility at Bhairahwa include site preparation, earth work, warehouse construction, administrative buildings, and electrical systems. (4) Procurement of equipment for three ICDs. (5) Internationally experienced consultants to supervise the construction and improvement of the three ICDs. (6) Technical assistance and training to reduce transport and Trade costs will simplify documents and procedures; modernize carrier's liability, insurance provisions, and customs policies and procedures; strengthen freight forwarding and customs clearing; and update foreign-currency trade regulations. (7) Installation of the Automated Systems for Customs Data. (8) Installation of the Advanced Cargo Information System. (9) Repayment of the Project Preparation Facility.
