Projet : Health Sector Support Additional Financing
Identifiant : P128663
Pays : Kenya (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 56.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 78 mois
Année d'approbation : 2011
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the Project are to improve (i) the delivery of quality essential health and nutrition services and utilization bywomen and children especially among the poor and drought affected populations; and (ii) the effectiveness of planning, financing, and procurement of pharmaceutical and medical supplies.
