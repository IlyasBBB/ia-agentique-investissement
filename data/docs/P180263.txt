Projet : Health System Efficiency and Resilience Project
Identifiant : P180263
Pays : West Bank and Gaza (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 10.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 62 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To support the Palestinian Authority in improving the quality, efficiency, and resilience of public health service delivery.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Health System Efficiency and Resilience Project for West Bank and Gaza is to support the Palestinian Authority (PA) in improving the quality, efficiency, and resilience of public health service delivery. The project comprises of four components. The first component, scaling up cost-effective public primary health care (PHC) services will increase the availability and quality of public PHC services. It consists of following sub-components: (i) delivery of noncommunicable diseases (NCD) prevention and control services through public PHC centers; and (ii) strengthening information systems and quality of PHC. The second component, improving public hospital service delivery will complement Palestinian Ministry of Health (PMOH) efforts to improve public hospital capacity in the West Bank and Gaza. It consists of following sub-components: (i) purchasing of medical equipment to expand hospital capacity in high-need areas; and (ii) strengthening management and quality of care in hospitals. The third component, project implementation and monitoring will finance necessary human resources and running costs for the project management unit (PMU) at the PMOH. The fourth component, contingent emergency response component (CERC) will improve the PA’s ability to respond effectively in the event of an emergency in line with World Bank procedures on disaster prevention and preparedness.
