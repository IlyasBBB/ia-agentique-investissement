Projet : Philippines First Digital Transformation Development Policy Financing
Identifiant : P179361
Pays : Philippines (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 600.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 15 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The program development objective (PDO) of this programmatic operation is to support Government reforms to foster an enabling environment for greater digital technology adoption by (a) improving digital transformation of government and digital infrastructure policies, (b) expanding financial inclusion through digital finance and (c) boosting business growth in digital services.

Résumé :
The development objective of the First Digital Transformation Development Policy Financing for Philippines is to support government reforms to foster an enabling environment for greater digital technology adoption by: (a) improving digital transformation of government and digital infrastructure policies, (b) expanding financial inclusion through digital finance, and (c) boosting business growth in digital services. The development policy loans (DPL) series builds on the nation’s long-term vision articulated in AmBisyon Natin 2040 and the recently launched Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028 to achieve inclusive growth and supports the government’s reform agenda in accelerating digitalization.
