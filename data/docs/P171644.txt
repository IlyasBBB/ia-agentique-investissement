Projet : Yangtze River Protection and Ecological Restoration Program
Identifiant : P171644
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 13052.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 66 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve institutional coordination, enhance ecological protection and reduce water pollution loads in select regions of the Yangtze River Basin

Résumé :
The development objective of the Yangtze River Protection and Ecological Restoration Program for China is to improve institutional coordination, enhance ecological protection and reduce water pollution loads in select regions of the Yangtze river basin. The program-for-results (PforR) results areas (RA) are the following: RA 1: improving institutions and innovations (provincial level); RA 2: advancing ecological protection through integrated river basin management (sub-basin level); and RA 3: reducing water pollution and transmission of plastic waste (county level).
