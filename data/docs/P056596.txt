Projet : CN-Shijiazhuang Urban Transport
Identifiant : P056596
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 286.2 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 93 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project objective is to foster the developme nt of an efficient and environmentally sustainable urban transport system in the city of Shijiazhuang while p roviding a wider set of travel choices for users. The sub-objectives are as follows:1. to develop a stronger and more efficient urban road administration;2. to improve the efficiency and safety of the road syste m for all modes of transport while preserving the benefits of the bicycle;3. to develop stronger and more effective public transport services;4. to improve the quality and efficiency of road maintenance activiti es in the city;5. to promote better environmental p rotection and environmental improvements; and6. to strengthen the institutions for managing transport planning, services and operations.

Résumé :
The Shijiazhuang Urban Transport Project will foster the development of an efficient, and environmentally sustainable urban transport system in the city, and provide as well a wider set of commuting choices to its users. The components are: 1) strengthening the primary, and secondary road networks within the second ring, including construction of interchanges, and railway overpasses/underpasses; and, completion, and widening of existing roads; 2) provision of additional facilities for bicycle users, and pedestrians alike; application of modern traffic engineering techniques, mainly within the first ring road; application of road safety measures; and, provision of parking facilities; 3) establishment of computer systems to provide bus management information, including an improved bus-stop program. In addition, bus parking, and maintenance depots, and station terminals for railways, and buses will be constructed; 4) financing of road maintenance works, and equipment, including a road maintenance planning system. Engineering designs, local construction supervision, and administration will be financed by the municipal government; 5) development of environmental monitoring, noise abatement, and emissions control programs; and, 6) strengthening technical skills, agency management and coordination, through technical assistance, training, and consulting services.
