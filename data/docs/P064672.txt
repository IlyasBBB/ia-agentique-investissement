Résumé :
The Basic Education Project will improve the efficiency, quality and equity of basic education in Nicaragua (pre-primary and grades 1 to 6) by reducing the repetition and dropout rates, and enhancing student academic achievement. The project focuses primarily on pre-primary and primary education where the vast majority of students come from poor segments of the population. The project will achieve its goal by the decentralization of educational management; development and distribution of educational materials, especially textbooks; expansion and improvement of non-formal pre-primary education; and rehabilitation, replacement, and repair of inadequate school plants.
