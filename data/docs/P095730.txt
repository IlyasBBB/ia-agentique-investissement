Projet : Fourth Poverty Reduction Support Credit
Identifiant : P095730
Pays : Ghana (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 140.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 14 mois
Année d'approbation : 2006
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed operation is a Poverty Reduction Support Credit (PRSC) that would build on reforms carried out by the Government of Ghana and support the implementation of the Ghana Poverty Reduction Strategy II(GPRS-II). The proposed PRSC-4 will support the policies and reforms aimed at improving the living conditions of the population by promoting growth and employment, accelerating human development, and strengthening governance and public sector management as spelled out in the GPRS-II. In doing so, the fourth PRSC would continue the support provided under the first series of PRSCs aimed at helping the country attack poverty in a comprehensive manner.

Résumé :
The fourth poverty reduction support credit (PRSC-4) for Ghana will focus on three broad components of the new poverty reduction agenda organized under: (i) accelerated private sector-led growth, (ii) vigorous human resource development, and (iii) good governance and civic responsibility. While the PRSC-4 support will translate primarily into funding for the implementation of the GPRS II, particular attention is given leveraging the poverty emphasis of several sector focused operations by ensuring appropriate expenditure allocation (level and structure) and by addressing some of the financing implications of proposed policy actions. The PRSC-4 also focuses on cross-cutting issues related to private sector development and the strengthening of institutions, ranging from issues related to governance, public sector reform, decentralization and public financial management.
