Projet : CAADP MDTF: Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) Child Trust Fund
Identifiant : P121899
Pays : Africa (Afrique)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 4.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 64 mois
Année d'approbation : 2010
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The original objective of the Project was to improve strategic planning and implementation of agricultural investments at national and regional levels. The grant’s PDO and results framework were restructured as part of the additional financing. The revised PDO is “improved enabling environment for African agricultural programs and policies at national and regional level.”
