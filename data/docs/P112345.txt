Projet : Additional Financing for Third Social Fund for Development Project
Identifiant : P112345
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 10.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 18 mois
Année d'approbation : 2008
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective (PDO) will remain the same, namely: to improve the range of services and economic opportunities available to the poorer segments of the population through the carrying out of community development, micro-finance and capacity building programs.The additional financing will scale up activities in Components 1 and 3 of the SFD III and these activities will be implemented under two new components: 4 and 5 as described below:-Component 4. Community-based Labor-intensive Works. This component is expected to transfer cash (for work) to 8,000-10,000 households within the most seriously affected communities. It is expected to transfer cash to help mitigate the impact of increased food prices through temporary work opportunities. The Program will also provide needed basic infrastructure to these communities in the fields of: irrigation, water harvesting, soil protection, agricultural terraces rehabilitation, maintenance and improvement of village access earth roads, streets pavement, forestation and other types of labor intensive based on the demand and priority needs of each community.-Component 5. Capacity Building of the Social Welfare Fund. This component will support: (i) a national survey to identify the poorest and most vulnerable in the society in order to improve targeting and expand the program; and (ii) complete a revised targeting system increasing the share of cash transfers received by the poorest beneficiaries to improve the ability of the program to reduce poverty immediately. The support to SWF will complement the technical assistance being provided by the European Commission.
