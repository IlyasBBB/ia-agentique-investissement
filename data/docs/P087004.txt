Projet : CV Road Sector Support Project
Identifiant : P087004
Pays : Cabo Verde (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 263.9 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 97 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase the mobility of the population in targeted areas and improve the planning and financing of the Road Sector.

Résumé :
The Road Sector Support Project for Cape Verde is intended to enhance the Borrower's road sector management. This will be achieved through: 1) institutional reform of the road sector and improved functioning of related civil works markets; and 2) better access to social and economic opportunities due to improved mobility for affected populations. The project has two main components: Component 1 supports the Government's program for reforming management of the road sector, and provides support for the capacity enhancement of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. The project also supports policy and strategy formulation and implementation for the transport sector in general through continuous sector dialogue. Component 2 supports priority road network improvements, including road upgrading or small bridge construction, and repairing key links.
