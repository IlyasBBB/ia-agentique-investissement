Résumé :
The main development objective of the Azerbaijan Second Highway Project is to reduce road transport costs and improve access, transit, and road safety within Azerbaijan's East-West and North-South corridors, through upgrading some sections of the Alat-Masalli road of the Baku-Iran highway (M3) and rehabilitating the Baku-Shamakhi road (M4). There are two project components: Component 1 upgrades some sections of the M3 highway between Alat and Masalli and rehabilitates the existing M4 road Baku-Shamakhi, as well as some access roads. Component 2 provides technical assistance, training, and goods to support strengthening of the transport ministry and the Road Transport Service Department and RTSD's road maintenance capacity and project implementation.
