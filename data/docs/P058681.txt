Projet : Education Sector Support Project
Identifiant : P058681
Pays : Malaysia (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 362.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT WILL COUNTER THE NEGATIVE EFFECTS TO HUMAN RESOURCEDEVELOPMENT BROUGHT ABOUT BY THE REGIONAL FINANCIAL CRISIS, ANDFURTHER THE REFORMS IN THE HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM TO ENABLE IT TODELIVER QUALITY EDUCATION IN A MORE EFFICIENT AND EQUITABLE MANNER.

Résumé :
The Education Sector Support Project follows the sector objectives of the Country Assistance Strategy (CAS) dated March 1999, and will assist the Government in restoring expenditure priorities in education. The CAS highlights the need for improved skills in science, mathematics, and information technology. The development objectives of the project address the negative effects of the economic downturn on the education sector, and assist in the medium-term economic recovery, through the development of cost-effective technical skills. The main components of the project include: 1) The improvement of basic education through capacity expansion and staff development, including the construction of schools, equipment supply, and teacher training/upgrading at various leveles. 2) The capacity expansion of the polytechnic system enrollment, and the quality enhancement of its training programs. This also includes construction, equipment supply, staff training, curriculumdevelopment, and polytechnica management and management systems improvement. 3) Support to the Ministry of Education (MOE) through development plans for institutional strengthening consisting of computer supply, technical assistance, and training for multiple databases integration; MOE staff development plans; institutional and staff development plans for higher education; and project management assistance for MOE staff.
