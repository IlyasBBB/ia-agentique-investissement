Projet : Emergency Recovery & Disaster Management Project
Identifiant : P070430
Pays : St. Lucia (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 7.68 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 59 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
This Project is part (phase 1 along with St Kitts and Nevis and Dominica) of the Emergency Recovery and Disaster Management Program (APL) for OECS countries, which includes 3 phases over 6 years, including contingent financing for future emergencies. Phase 2 includes Grenada, and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. The program/project comprises two main components: 1) physical mitigation and prevention investments for pre-disaster strengthening of public infrastructure as well as post-disaster reconstruction of public infrastructure; and 2) institutional strengthening of disaster preparedness and management capacity.
