Projet : MARKET TOWNS DEV
Identifiant : P000722
Pays : Ethiopia (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 52.9 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 112 mois
Année d'approbation : 1990
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
TO SUPPORT GOVERNMENT'S URBAN OBJECTIVES IN ITS 3 YR PLAN (1986-89) AND TO INCREASE EFFICIENCY OF KEY URBAN CENTERS WHICH ARE MARKET AND SERVICE CENTERS FOR THE AGRICULTURAL HINTERLAND THROUGH PRIORITY INVESTMENTS, STRENGTHENING MUNICIPAL ADMIN AND FINANCE AND SUPPORT TO SMALL SCALE ENTERPRISES.

Résumé :
The project would improve basic infrastructure and living conditions in project towns, strengthen the capacity of the Ministry of Urban Development and Housing (MUDH), provide assistance to urban service agencies, encourage micro- and small-scale enterprises in project towns, introduce an experimental income enhancement/entrepreneurship development program for women, and promote the adoption of sound cost recovery policies and practices. The project includes support for (a) sites and services and housing development in six phase one project towns; (b) improvements to municipal infrastructure and agricultural markets and provision of essential municipal vehicles and equipment in phase one project towns, and improvements to markets and related infrastructure in phase two project towns; and (c) major extensions of water supply systems in two phase one project towns and rehabilitation works in four phase one project towns. It also supports: (d) credit lines and technical assistance to promote micro- and small-scale enterprise development in phase one and phase two project towns; and (c) institutional support programs to strengthen management and maintenance systems, and assistance for project management and implementation as appropriate, for project agencies.
