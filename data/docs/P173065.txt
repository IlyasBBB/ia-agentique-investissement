Projet : Financing Locally-Led Climate Action Program
Identifiant : P173065
Pays : Kenya (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 251.4 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 62 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The PDO is to deliver locally-led climate resilience actions and strengthen county and national governments' capacity to manage climate risks

Résumé :
This program paper proposes an Additional Financing (AF) and restructuring comprising: AF of 30 million dollars in IDA resources including 28.35 million dollars for DLI 2 and 1.65 million dollars for national level activities under the IPF (category 3). As a result, there will be changes to the Program’s disbursement estimates; (b) restructuring of the Program to reflect: (i) changes to results framework indicators at the PDO and intermediate result levels, namely removal, addition, and revision of indicators and adjustments to several annual targets; and (ii) changes to the disbursement estimates and reallocation between disbursement categories, namely transfer of unused US$0.4 million from DLI1 to DLI2, and reallocation of 1,558,421 dollars under the program preparation advance to the IPF component (category 3).
