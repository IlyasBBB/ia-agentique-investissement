Projet : MA-Second Capital Market Development and SME Finance DPL
Identifiant : P153603
Pays : Morocco (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 350.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 11 mois
Année d'approbation : 2017
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The program development objectives of this DPL are to:Pillar A. Improve access to finance for small and young enterprisesPillarB. Strengthen capital markets by improving the institutional framework and broadening the range of instrumentsPillar C. Improve the financial sustainability of the Caisse Marocaine des RetraitesPillar D. Strengthen oversight of the banking sector.This is the second in a series of two development policy loans (DPLs). The proposed operation supports the long-standing commitment of Moroccan authorities to develop a more inclusive and diversified financial sector. The series supports comprehensive reforms to enable new financing solutions for economic and social infrastructure and small enterprises. In this process, the financial sector takes on more risk, so the program features policies that balance risk-taking with stronger oversight by the regulators. Finally, the reform of the largest pension fund in Morocco ensures that it remains a large local investor in existing and new asset classes. The following institutions in Morocco are involved in the operation: Ministry of Economy and Finance, Bank Al-Maghrib (Central Bank), L’Autorite Marocaine du Marche des Capitaux (capital market authority), and the Autorite de Controle des Assurances et de la Prevoyance Sociale (independent insurance and pension supervisor). This ISR covers results indicators to March 30 2018 (up to project closing). They were provided to the WBG by the Ministry of Economy and Finance on May 31, 2018. Results indicator for final evaluation are tracked to end of calendar year 2018.

Résumé :
This program document for the Second Capital Market Development and Small and Medium-Size Enterprise Finance Development Policy Loan Project has four pillars as follows : (i) Pillar A - Improve access to finance for small and young enterprises; (ii) Pillar B - Strengthen capital markets by improving the institutional framework and broadening the range of instruments; (iii) Pillar C - Improve the financial sustainability of the Caisse Marocaine des Retraites; and (iv) Pillar D - Strengthen oversight of the banking sector. This program supports a private-sector-driven economic model, as well as resilience by helping micro, small, and medium-size enterprises (MSMEs) recover from the European Union’s (EU) economic slump and other recent economic challenges. The program is aligned with the World Bank Group’s Strategy for Middle East and North Africa, which sets an agenda of using development to promote peace and social stability.
