Projet : Power Sector Priority Investments
Identifiant : P087801
Pays : Timor-Leste (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 1.41 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 44 mois
Année d'approbation : 2004
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is the delivery of least-cost, high-quality electricity service with minimum dependence on the government's budget.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Power Sector Priority Investments Project, is the delivery of least-cost, high-quality electricity service, with minimum dependence on the government's budget. This objective will be attained through improvements in the Timor Leste Electricity (EDTL) generation capacity, generation and distribution efficiency, and, through savings achieved through demand reductions in the use of energy efficient lamps and light bulbs. To this end, project components aim: 1) to enhance base load generation capacity, and reduce fuel costs, the unit engine at the Comoro Power Station will be replaced with a new 2.8 MW unit; 2). to improve service quality, and reduce losses the distribution system at Dili will be reinforced, and rehabilitated; 3) to achieve reductions in fuel costs incurred by EDTL, the distribution, and installation of efficient lamps and light bulbs should ensure greater efficiency in energy end-use for lighting; and, 4) project management activities will support auditing, procurement, and financial management.
