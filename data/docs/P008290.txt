Pays : Belarus (Europe et Asie centrale)

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE OBJECTIVE IS TO INITIATE PROGRAMS TO CONSERVE AND MANAGE THEBIODIVERSITY OF KEY ENDANGERED FORESTS, LINKING SUCH EFFORTS WITHONGOING GEF-SUPPORTED WORK IN THE ADJOING BIALOWIEZA PRIMEVAL FORESTIN POLAND. THE PROJECT INCORPORATES BOTH IN-SITU AND EX-SITU OPTIONSFOR CONSERVING BIODIVERSITY. THIS ENTAILS SCIENTIFIC STUDY OF THEFLORA AND FAUNA, THREATS TO THEIR VIABILITY FROM HUMAN PRESSURES ANDDETAILING OPTIONS TO ENSURE THE CONSERVATION OF SPECIES.
