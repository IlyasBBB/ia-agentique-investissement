Projet : Additional Financing to the Agriculture and Natural Resources Landscape Management Project (SUSTENTA)
Identifiant : P168940
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 60.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 52 mois
Année d'approbation : 2019
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is to integrate rural households into sustainable agriculture and forest-based value chains in the Project area and, in the event of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, to provide immediate and effective response to said Eligible Crisis or Emergency.
