Projet : Early Childhood Development Project
Identifiant : P147074
Pays : Jamaica (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 14.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 56 mois
Année d'approbation : 2014
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project endorses the objectives of the National Strategic Plan. Its development objectives are to: (i) improve parenting education and support programs; (ii) improve monitoring of children’s development, the screening of household level risks, and the risk mitigation and early intervention systems, (iii) enhance the quality of early childhood schools and care facilities; and (iv) strengthen early childhood organizations and institutions.
