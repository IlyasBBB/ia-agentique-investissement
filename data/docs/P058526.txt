Projet : Integrated Drug Prevention Pilot Project
Identifiant : P058526
Pays : Argentina (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 6.9 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 32 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THIS LIL WILL (A)REDUCE DEMAND FOR ILLICIT DRUGS THROUGH STRENGTHENINGOF PUBLIC & PRIVATE DRUG ABUSE PREVENTION INSTITUTIONS;(B) SUPPORT INFORMATION GATHERING TO ASSESS THE PREVALENCE & FACTORSASSOCIATED WITH DRUG ABUSE. PARTICIPATORY MECHANISM, TARGETING VULNER-ABLE GROUPS(I.E. YOUTHS) WOULD BE USED TO ESTABLISH CAPACITY IN DRUGABUSE PREVENTION, TREATMENT, REHABILITATION & REINTEGRATION OF ADDICTSINTO SOCIETY.

Résumé :
The Integrated Drug Prevention Pilot Project seeks to test the applicability of a multisectoral drug prevention strategy in the Argentine context. There are three project components. 1) Participatory approaches to drug prevention will promote local solutions to drug use in selected municipalities on a pilot basis via training local community members and sector professionals; training youth leaders; and financing the implementation of the municipal plans for drug prevention. 2) Information systems, monitoring, and evaluation will establish the network to facilitate generation and exchange of information relevant to drug prevention, as well as to carry out the monitoring and evaluation of the project by means of a) a survey-based epidemiological study to obtain information on drug consumption patterns; b) development of prevention methods and training modules that will be disseminated via national, regional, and provincial workshops; c) creation of a network of monitoring centers to strengthen existing institutions focused on drug prevention; and d) monitoring and evaluation of overall project implementation processes. 3) Project management will set up a project implementation unit.
