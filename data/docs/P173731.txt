Projet : Somalia Recurrent Cost & Reform Financing Project - Phase 3
Identifiant : P173731
Pays : Somalia, Federal Republic of (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 68.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 91 mois
Année d'approbation : 2020
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To support the Federal Government of Somalia and Eligible Federal Member States to strengthen resource management systems, the inter-governmental fiscal framework, and service delivery systems in health and education.

Résumé :
The parent project and additional financing support Somalia’s efforts to strengthen core government systems and intergovernmental fiscal arrangements underpinning the delivery of basic services. Through performance‑based conditions, the project incentivizes the Federal Government of Somalia and Federal Member States to implement and sustain priority governance and fiscal reforms. It focuses on strengthening intergovernmental fiscal relations, enhancing institutional capacity, and supporting the financing and delivery of essential health and education services. The project also incorporates mechanisms to strengthen accountability by incorporating citizen feedback into service delivery and reform implementation.The third additional financing increases the allocation for Component 2 by US$12 million and extends the closing date to December 31, 2027. Through Performance‑Based Conditions, the additional financing supports improvements in the reliability of Federal Memeber States' payroll systems.
