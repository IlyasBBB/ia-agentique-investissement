Projet : National Transmission Grid Project
Identifiant : P048852
Pays : Turkiye (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 502.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 115 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
In October 2001, TEAS was split into three companies: a transmission company (TEIAS), a trading company (TETTAS), and a generating company (EUAS) in accordance with one of the covenants of the loan. Since TEAS was the original borrower under the the National Transmission Grid Project/Loan (NTGP), the Loan had to be restructured.This occurred in January 2002 the loan was renegotiated and two loan assumption agreements were created, one with TEIAS for $250 Million and one with the Government for $20 Million. The Loan with TEIAS funds transmission facilities and assistance in establishing TEIAS as an independent transmission company and system and market operator. The $20 Million Loan to the Government is used to help fund technical assistance to establish an open competitive electricity market in Turkey -- these activities include: (a) Assistance in setting up the Energy Market Regulatory Authority (EMRA) - the independent energy regulator for the country. (b) Assistance in restructuring the generation and distribution sectors (EUAS and TEDAS) into commercially viable entities that can be corporatized and privatized. (c) Assistance in establishing the Trading Company (TETAS) as the holder of private power purchase contracts, and the assignment of initial vesting contracts with distributors. (d) Assistance to the Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources (MENR).In the course of the restructuring the project development objectives were altered to some extent. The updated and expanded development objectives are to assist with: (a) implementing the new Electricity Market Law and the restructuring of the power sector; (b) establishing TEIAS as an efficient, low cost transmission company, technical and market system operator; (c) developing adequate transmission capacity in a timely and environmentally sustainable manner; and (d) establishing the financial viability of TEIAS.

Résumé :
The objectives of the National Transmission Grid Project are: (a) the development of adequate transmission capability in a timely manner; (b) establishment of the independent operation of the transmission grid system; and (c) maintenance of the financial viability of the state institution responsible for grid development and operation. The project components include: (a) development of a three-year time-slice of Turkish Electricity Generation and Transmission Corporation's (TEAS) grid investments (transmission lines, substations and load dispatch facilities) for the period 1999 through 2002; and (b) technical assistance for: (i) establishment of the independent operation of the transmission grid system; (ii) institutional development including environmental analysis of grid investments; and (iii) helping TEAS borrow from the international markets.
