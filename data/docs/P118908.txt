Projet : TF Philippines Local Government Grants for Sanitation Pilots
Identifiant : P118908
Pays : Philippines (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 1.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 25 mois
Année d'approbation : 2009
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The aim of the project is to generate lessons for national policy and program development on implementing intermediate sanitation solutions within a broader framework of local sanitation assistance in six trial sites.At the local level, the desired outcome is the reduction of sanitation-related disease and water pollution in six program sites as a result of participating local governments making better sustainable sanitation investments and strengthening capacity in the implementation and management of sanitation projects through trial implementation.At the national level, the desired outcome is that the national government, led by the Departments of Health and Environment and Natural Resources, are able to translate the lessons from these pilots into national policy/program.The outputs expected are at least four sanitation infrastructure projects implemented by participating local governments within an overall local sanitation plan and program framework by 2010 and lessons learned documentation through seminars, toolkits and other materials.
