Projet : Environmental Remediation Pilot Project
Identifiant : P033965
Pays : Bulgaria (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 25.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 56 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
LENDING TO GOVERNMENT FOR ADDRESING LIABILITIES OF MITIGATING PASTENVIRONMENTAL DAMAGES AT THE MDK COPPER SMELTER.

Résumé :
The main object of the Environmental Remediation Pilot Project is to support the Government of Bulgaria in (i) reducing environmental hazards caused by past pollution and unsafe environmental management practices at MDK Copper Smelter (MDK); (ii) facilitating private investments in the company; and (iii) contributing to improvements in the environmental performance of the plant. This pilot project is envisaged to become a model for addressing past environmental damages and environmental damages and environmental liabilities in the process of privatization. The components include a emergency remediation program and the remediation of past environmental damages.
