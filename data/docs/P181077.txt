Projet : SECOND SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURAL INTENSIFICATION AND FOOD SECURITY PROJECT
Identifiant : P181077
Pays : Rwanda (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 20.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 3 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase agricultural productivity, market access, and food security of the targeted beneficiaries in the project areas.

Résumé :
The development objective of Second Sustainable Agricultural Intensification and Food Security Project for Rwanda is to increase agricultural productivity, market access, and food security of the targeted beneficiaries in the project areas. This project has four components. 1) The first component, Institutional Strengthening, Agriculture Productivity Enhancement, and Nutrition Improvement, has the following sub-components: (i) Strengthening farmers’ organizations; (ii) Agricultural productivity enhancement; and (iii) Improving nutrition outcomes at household level. 2) The second component, Irrigation and Water Use Efficiency, has the following sub-components: (i) Support Climate Smart and Efficient Irrigation Interventions; and (ii) Strengthen irrigation management capacity. 3) The third component, Market Linkages and Value Addition Investments Support, has the following sub-components: (i) Capacity building to foster market linkages; and (ii) Investment support to market linkages. 4) The fourth component, Project Management and Technical Assistance, has the following sub-components: (i) Project management; (ii) Knowledge Management and Dissemination; and (iii) Technical Assistance.
