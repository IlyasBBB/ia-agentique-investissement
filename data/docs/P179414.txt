Projet : National Youth Opportunities Towards Advancement Project
Identifiant : P179414
Pays : Kenya (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 200.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 67 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase employment, earnings and promote savings for targeted youth, at national scale

Résumé :
The development objective of the National Youth Opportunities Towards Advancement Project for Kenya is to increase employment, earnings and promote savings for targeted youth, at national scale. The project comprises of four components. The first component, improving youth employability will connect targeted youth to better employment opportunities by providing them with training, on-the-job experience, and access to intermediation services. It consists of following sub-components: (i) provision of training and work experience; and (ii) operationalization of labor market observatory and supporting intermediation. The second component, expanding employment opportunities aims to expand employment opportunities through entrepreneurship development and support to social enterprises. It consists of following sub-components: (i) support for entrepreneurship; and (ii) results-based financing partnership with social enterprises. The third component, supporting youth savings will provide savings opportunities to the beneficiaries of components 1 and 2. It consists of following sub-components: (i) supporting savings through monetary incentives and nudges; and (ii) enhancing national social security fund (NSSF) operations by upgrading processes, systems, and communications. The fourth component, strengthening youth employment systems, capacity, and project management will strengthen the systems and capacity of national and county-level implementing agencies, and finance project management activities. It consists of following sub-components: (i) build county government capacity to invest in youth employment; (ii) monitoring and evaluation and delivery systems; and (iii) project management and coordination.
