Projet : Montenegro Environmental Infrastructure
Identifiant : P074618
Pays : Serbia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 2.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 34 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The overall objectives of the Project are to improve water supply and sanitation in Montenegro, the smaller of the two Republics comprising FRY. Specifically, the Project would aim to improve environmental conditions in Montenegro's coastal region and the quality of water supply in the Zeta Valley, near the Republic's capital city, Podgorica. The Project Development Objectives will be achieved through: (a) the rehabilitation of key environmental infrastructure in several coastal municipalities in the region of Kotor Bay, a prime tourist zone; and (b) the completion of a water supply pipeline to replace contaminated water sources in the Zeta Valley. The Project will also provide a small amount of technical assistance for engineering services and auditing. The total project costs will be US$2.8 million, of which US$2.0 million will be Bank financed through a grant

Résumé :
The Montenegro Environmental Infrastructure Project aims at improving the environmental conditions in the coastal region of Montenegro, as well as the quality of water supply in, and around the capital city of Podgorica. The project components will: 1) finance the rehabilitation of the multi-municipal Kotor landfill site, to meet sanitary standards. Such rehabilitation will comprise, other than a new fence, and gate, a groundwater monitoring system, leakage treatment, and a gas collecting system. A new compactor and bulldozer will be provided, to allow an adequate landfill operation, while drainage systems will be installed to control degassing, and drainage. In addition solid waste equipment will be financed to institute long-term environmental monitoring, maintain, and manage the landfill site; 2) finance the completion of the water supply pipeline construction, thus replacing contaminated water sources in the Zeta Valley, with water from the Podgorica municipal water supply system; and, 3) support project implementation, through the provision of funds for the completion of engineering services, and for auditing of project accounts.
