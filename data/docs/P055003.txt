Projet : Nutrition, Food Security, and Social Mobilization Project
Identifiant : P055003
Pays : Mauritania (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 5.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 74 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project objective is to evaluate the effectiveness of specific activities contained in two programs--one urban and one rural--to reduce child malnutrition in a measurable way. The main development objective is to improve the nutritional status of mothers and children, to promote the physical development of children, and to increase generating activities.

Résumé :
The objective of the Nutrition, Food Security, and Social Mobilization Project is to evaluate the effectiveness of specific activities combined into two different "prototypes" of programs, one urban and one rural; and to reduce in a measurable way child malnutrition in Mauritania. Components include: a) cooperative mobilization and rural radio for nutrition; b) nutrition counseling and intervention; c) micro-projects; d) social mobilization and rural radio; e) mobilization and urban integration for nutrition; f) selection NGOs; g) monitoring; h) external evaluation; and i) financial management, accounting, auditing and loan administration change initiative (LACI) implementation.
