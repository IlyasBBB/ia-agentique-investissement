Projet : GEF CR-ECOMARKETS
Identifiant : P061314
Pays : Costa Rica (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 8.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 76 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project's Development Objective is to increase forest conservation in Costa Rica by supporting the development of markets and private sector providers for environmental services supplied by privately-owned forests. The Global Objective with respect to Global Environment Facility (GEF) financing is to contribute to the long-term conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity outside of national parks and biological reserves in Costa Rica's portion of the Mesoamerican Biological Corridor. These objectives will be achieved in part the involvement of local individuals and organizations in conserving and sustainable managing forest ecosystems and supporting conservation-oriented poverty alleviation in rural areas.
