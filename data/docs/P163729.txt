Projet : Yemen Emergency Crisis Response Project - Second Additional Financing
Identifiant : P163729
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 200.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 58 mois
Année d'approbation : 2017
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective is to provide short-term employment and access to selected basic services to the most vulnerable; preserve existing implementation capacity of two service delivery programs; and provide emergency cash transfers to the poor and vulnerable in response to the food crisis.
