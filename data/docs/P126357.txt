Projet : Second Support to the Education Sector Project PASEN II
Identifiant : P126357
Pays : Nicaragua (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 32.95 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 80 mois
Année d'approbation : 2012
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the Project are to: (a) improve the students' retention rate in Primary Education Schools located in Participating Municipalities; and (b) strengthen MINED’s education management capacity.

Résumé :
The objective of the Project is to: (a) improve the students retention rate in primary education schools located in participating municipalities; and (b) strengthen Ministry of Education's (MINED's) education management capacity. Specifically, the Project focuses on forty municipalities in six departments, and the two Atlantic Autonomous Regions- South Atlantic Autonomous Region (RAAS) and North Atlantic Autonomous Region (RAAN). Project components are as following: improving the schools' learning environment; improving the quality of teaching methods; and strengthening MINED's education management capacity.
