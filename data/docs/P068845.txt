Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THIS ROLLING REINSTATABLE GUARANTEE, APPLICABLE ON BOTH INTEREST AND PRINCIPAL, WILL SUPPORT ARGENTINA'S ISSUANCE OF A SERIES OF SIX ZERO COUPON NOTES EACH FOR A FACE VALUE OF $250 MILLION, TO BE FULLY REPAID WITHIN A FIVE-YEAR PERIOD. WITH THE ISSUANCE OF THE NOTES AND THE GUARANTEE, ARGENTINA WILL CANCEL $250 MILLION FROM THE SECOND TRANCHE OF THE SSAL.
