Projet : Economic and Financial Governance Project
Identifiant : P180696
Pays : Djibouti (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 15.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 65 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Improve tax administration efficiency, improve transparency in expenditure management, and increase citizen access to digital services

Résumé :
Component 1: Strengthen Tax Administration (US$ 3.60 million),The objective of this component is to provide the technical assistance (TA) required to improve tax formulation and the efficiency of tax collection. It includes (i) Creation and operationalization of the tax policy unit (TPU); (ii) DGI capacity need assessment, (iii) Support to design and implement tax compliance improvement plan, and (iv) Implementation of the remaining ITAS modules including e-services (e-filling, e-declaration, and e-payments), and integrating the platform with other PFM systems.Component 2: Strengthen public expenditure management (US$ 3.91 million)The objective of this component is to support the Government of Djibouti's efforts to (i) modernize the budget preparation and execution processes by rendering them more efficient and transparent and updating the PFM reform strategy and action plan; and (ii) implement the recently adopted National Development Plan.Component 3: Strengthen oversight of public finances (US$ 1.24 million)The objective of this component is to increase transparency in public expenditure management through institutional capacity building. The project will finance technical assistance to reinforce the Supreme Audit Institution and support the operationalization of the SOE Oversight Entity (SEPE) reporting to the Ministry of Economy and Finance.Component 4: Project management (US$ 1.25 million)The Project will finance the operating cost of the Project Coordination Unit (PCU) located within ANSIE. The PCU will oversee the coordination and management of the project implementation. Activities to finance will include (i) the development of annual work programs and corresponding Procurement Plans (PPs), (ii) the management of fiduciary and monitoring activities, (iii) the coordination of technical work and the provision of support services to the specialized units within the relevant ministries, (iv) the monitoring of and reporting on the implementation of the project’s activities, (v) the payment of the activities related to project coordination and monitoring, as well as project audit. The project will support the establishment of institutional arrangements including an entity "Integrated PFM Reform Project Management Unit - (UGP- RGFP)" to oversee and coordinate all PFM reform activities in Djibouti.
