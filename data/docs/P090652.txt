Projet : Partnership for Polio Eradication Project Supplemental Credit
Identifiant : P090652
Pays : Nigeria (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 170.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 85 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Project Objective: This project is part of a multi-country program and will contribute to a key public good: the global eradication of Poliomyelitis by 2005 as aimed for by the World Health Organization.The overall goal of this project is to assist Nigeria in its efforts to eradicate Poliomyelitis. More specifically, the objective of this supplemental credit is to provide the additional polio vaccine required to carry out the intensification of Nigeria's ongoing polio eradication program during 2005 and 2006, needed because of the outbreaks of wild polio especially in but not limited to the North of the country.Key Performance Indicators: The project.s success would be measured by the following key indicators and targets:· Timely arrival at national level of OPV procured through UNICEF. Target: vaccines arrive at least 5 weeks before the SIA in the national strategic store in Abuja. Tool: UNICEF/NPI vaccine arrival report.· Coverage of SIA. Target: coverage of OPV is at least 80% in each endemic State. Tool: cluster sample survey according to a WHO approved methodology.Achievements measured by these indicators will be decisive for the IDA buy-down.Project description: The on-going IDA supported PPEP has three components: (i) the procurement of OPV; (ii) supplemental operations including cold chain, social mobilization and training; and (iii) surveillance. IDA finances the first component. The project is part of a multi-donor financed framework which has provided funding amounting US $ 157.1 million, of which IDA financed US $ 28.7 million for procurement of OPV.Rationale for the Supplemental CreditNigeria.s polio initiative is guided by ERC, a group comprised of global and national polio experts. Based on review of the surveillance data in August 2004, ERG concluded that Nigeria is making poor progress and is not on track to achieve polio eradication by 2005. Therefore, ERC recommended to intensify SIA efforts during 2004 and 2005. FGN and the ICC have endorsed the recommendations to intensify SIAs and have initiated actions to implement them.The proposed Supplemental Credit would finance the procurement of extra OPV required for additional SIAs in 2005. The proposed credit is consistent with the original project development objective and meets the requirement of OP13.20 on supplemental financing

Résumé :
The Partnership for Polio Eradication Project is part of a multi-country program, intended to achieve Poliomyelitis eradication by 2005. However, in 2003-2004, wild polio cases increased in Kano, and throughout northern Nigeria, spread to other Nigerian states, and reappeared in countries that had struggled for years to eliminate it. Because of the increase in wild polio cases, the eradication campaign for Nigeria desperately needs to be intensified, and several extra supplemental immunization activities (SIAs) need to be organized, as recommended, and additional oral polio vaccine (OPV) is still required to cover the needs for 2005 and 2006. The proposed supplemental credit is consistent with the original project development objective, and meets the requirement on supplemental financing, in compliance with the Articles of Agreement, and it is therefore recommended to approve this supplemental financing.
