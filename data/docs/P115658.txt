Projet : Second National Urban Water Sector Reform Project (Additional Financing)
Identifiant : P115658
Pays : Nigeria (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 120.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 47 mois
Année d'approbation : 2012
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project's Development Objectives are to: (i) improve the reliability of water supply produced in Cross River and Lagos States; (ii) increase access to piped water networks in Lagos State and in seven cities in Cross River State; and (iii) improve commercialviability of the urban water utilities in Cross River and Lagos States.
