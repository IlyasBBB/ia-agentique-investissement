Projet : PIVOT - Preventing Infections through Vigilance at the Origin of Transmission in Latin America and the Caribbean
Identifiant : P515690
Pays : Latin America and Caribbean (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 15.82 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 34 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Strengthen national and regional capacities within Latin America and the Caribbean to prevent, detect, and respond to zoonotic threats, aligned with a One Health approach.

Résumé :
The project is part of a larger Pandemic Fund financed regional entity proposal to support the Inter-American Institute for Regional Cooperation in Agriculture (IICA) to strengthen pandemic prevention, preparedness, and response capacities in Latin America and the Caribbean by reinforcing multisectoral collaboration at the human-animal-environment interface in order to reduce the risk of zoonotic outbreaks—particularly avian influenza—through a coordinated One Health Approach. The project will support IICA and its partners to strengthen regional governance and formal multisectoral continental coordination structures; enhance data integration and joint risk assessments, enabling countries to adapt to evolving threats; harmonize and modernize risk analysis, surveillance and genomic platforms across countries; and upgrade regional preparedness, biosecurity, and sustainable financing mechanisms for avian influenza and other high‑risk zoonotic threats.
