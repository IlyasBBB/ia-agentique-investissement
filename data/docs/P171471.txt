Résumé :
The development objectives of the Support for Transition to Labor Market Project for Turkey are to improve employability of Syrians under temporary protection (SuTP), international protection status holders and applicants (IPSHA), and Turkish citizens and to facilitate access to labor markets. The project comprises of two components. The first component, support for counseling services and employment support objective is to implement a menu of active labor market programs (ALMPs) and counseling activities to support integration of social assistance beneficiaries, SuTP and IPSHA, and Turkish citizens into the labor market. It consists of following sub-components: (i) counseling services and employment support via Turkish Employment Agency (ISKUR); and (ii) livelihood advisory services and referrals to other services via Turkish red crescent (TRC). The second component, support for delivery of employment services objective is to strengthen the capacities of the Ministry of Labor and Social Security (MoLSS), ISKUR, and TRC to implement the project and enhance and use monitoring and evaluation (M and E) data on the labor market and social assistance interventions. It consists of following sub-components: (i) M and E, data management, communication, and project management activities under MoLSS; (ii) M and E, data management, communication, and project management activities under ISKUR; and (iii) M and E, data management, communication, and project management activities under Kizilay (TRC).
