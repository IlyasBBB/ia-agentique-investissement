Projet : Punjab Affordable Housing Program
Identifiant : P173663
Pays : Pakistan (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 250.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 64 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To support the Government of Punjab in strengthening its housing institutions and systems, and enhancing the quantity and quality of affordable housing supply.

Résumé :
The Punjab Affordable Housing Program (PAHP), with a total financing of EUR 176.70 million (US$200 million equivalent) is designed to improve access to affordable housing in the Province of Punjab in Pakistan, through a) increasing the quantity and quality of housing supply, b) improving housing institutions and systems, and c) providing direct financial assistance to eligible low-income households. Addressing a substantial affordable housing deficit in Punjab, the Operation employs a hybrid financing approach using both Program for Results (PforR) and Investment Project Financing (IPF) modalities to deliver expected outcomes and results.
