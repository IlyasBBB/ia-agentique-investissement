Projet : Oil Institution Building TA Project
Identifiant : P044830
Pays : Georgia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 1.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 45 mois
Année d'approbation : 1997
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
FEASIBILITIY STUDY OF MAJOR EXPORT PIPELINE FROM BAKU TO SUPSA ON GEORGIAN BLACK SEA COAST TO ENHANCE GOVERNMENT CAPACITY TO EVALUATE PIPELINE ROUTING OPTIONS IN THE SUBREGION AND NEGOTIATE TRANSIT TERMS. PROJECT WILL ALSO PROVIDE MORE GENERAL INSTITUTION-BUILDING SUPPORT TO GIOC, STATE-OWNED ENTERPRISE ESTABLISHED PRIMARILY TO EFFECT TRANSIT OF CENTRAL ASIAN OIL THROUGH GEORGIA.
