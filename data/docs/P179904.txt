Projet : Northern Uganda Social Action Fund IV Project
Identifiant : P179904
Pays : Uganda (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 250.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 81 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To support sustainable livelihoods, to enhance poor and vulnerable households’ resilience to shocks and to strengthen adaptive social protection systems in Uganda.

Résumé :
The objective of the Fourth Northern Uganda Social Action Fund Project is to support sustainable livelihoods, enhance poor and vulnerable households’ resilience to shocks, and strengthen adaptive social protection systems in Uganda. The project has five components, the first being sustainable livelihood enhancement. This component includes two subcomponents: sustainable livelihood enhancement in rural areas and entrepreneurship support in urban and peri-urban areas. The second component is enhanced resilience and human capital development. This component includes two subcomponents: enhanced resilience and human capital development, and disaster risk financing for shock-responsive safety nets. The third component is the transformational delivery system. This component includes four subcomponents: national social registry, scaling up digital delivery systems, data-driven planning, monitoring, and evaluation, and strengthening transparency, accountability, and anti-corruption. The fourth component is the project management. This component will ensure that the activities supported through the project are coordinated and well-implemented. Finally, the fifth component is the contingent emergency response component.
