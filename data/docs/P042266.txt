Projet : Teacher Education and Teacher Deployment
Identifiant : P042266
Pays : Sri Lanka (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 79.3 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 113 mois
Année d'approbation : 1996
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve the quality, cost-effectiveness and coverage of education in Sri Lanka. This will be achieved by a) improving education attainment and equity, by having better-trained teachers widely available in schools, particularly disadvantaged schools, to have improved teaching/learning; b)making the system more efficient by rationalizing teacher recruitment, deployment and training policies; c) making more effective use of education expenditure through rationalizing teacher numbers and their deployment, enabling savings on salary costs to be used for quality expenditures; d)improving the quality of teacher education; and e)making the teacher training system more cost-effective.

Résumé :
The objectives of the Teacher Education and Teacher Deployment Project, based on policy initiatives agreed between the government and IDA over the past year, are to improve the quality, cost-effectiveness and coverage of education in Sri Lanka, by: 1) improving education attainment and equity; 2) making the system more efficient; 3) making more effective use of education expenditure; 4) improving the quality of teacher education; and 5) making the teacher training system more cost-effective. The project will consist of the following components: 1) rationalization of teacher deployment; 2) rationalization of structure and organization of teacher education; 3) upgrading of teacher education programs; 4) strengthening of staff and management; 5) strengthening and upgrading of the teacher training institutions; 6) strengthening the management of teacher training institutions; 7) studies and monitoring; and 8) project coordination.
