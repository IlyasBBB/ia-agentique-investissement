Projet : Pakistan Public Resources for Inclusive Development Multiphase Programmatic Approach: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Identifiant : P515269
Pays : Pakistan (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 650.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 60 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Program Development Objective is to strengthen the fiscal system to support macroeconomic stability and service delivery.

Résumé :
The proposed KP PRID addresses constraints in OSR mobilization, PFM, service delivery, and the data ecosystem. By increasing revenue, improving expenditure quality, and strengthening data systems, it will support fiscal sustainability and provincial service delivery. The Program will achieve the PDO through three Results Areas (RAs). RA1 will strengthen tax policy and administration by improving compliance and broadening the tax base, particularly for STS and AIT. RA2 will improve non-salary school budgets, align healthcare budgets with population needs, and advance broader PFM reforms in budgeting, digitization, resource flows, transparency, and accountability. RA3 will strengthen the data ecosystem to provide timely, reliable information for fiscal policy, expenditure prioritization, and strategic planning.
