Projet : Emergency Locust Response Program Phase 1 Ethiopia Additional Finance
Identifiant : P178434
Pays : Eastern and Southern Africa (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 60.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 55 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To respond to the threat posed by the locust outbreak and to strengthen systems for preparedness.

Résumé :
In response to a desert locust plague threatening the livelihoods and food security of millions in the Horn of Africa and the Middle East and North Africa, this project will finance swarm control, livelihood protection and rehabilitation, and strengthen swarm identification and early response system of the affected countries.
