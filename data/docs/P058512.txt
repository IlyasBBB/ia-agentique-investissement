Projet : Education Development Project
Identifiant : P058512
Pays : Bosnia and Herzegovina (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 14.6 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 55 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT HAS THE FOLLOWING OBJECTIVES: (1) TO IMPROVE CHILDREN'SLEARNING SKILLS IN PRIMARY EDUCATION BY UPGRADING THE TEACHING METHOD-OLOGY OF BIH'S 15,000 PRIMARY SCHOOL TEACHERS, AND (2) TO PROMOTE THEEFFICIENT USE OF SCARCE PUBLIC RESOURCES FOR EDUCATION IN BIH BY PRO-VIDING PUBLIC POLICY MAKERS AND DECISION MAKERS WITH MANAGEMENT TOOLSAND INFORMATION NECESSARY TO MEASURE INPUTS, OUTPUTS AND OUTCOMES INTHE EDUCATION SYSTEM.

Résumé :
The Education Development Project, will improve teaching, and learning processes in Bosnia and Herzegovina, therefore improve the quality of primary education, and will promote equitable use, and coordination of public resources to build a professional basis for the education system. The components will: 1) establish a Quality Fund to finance school grants, teacher training grants, and scholarships, and grants to support institutional strengthening of those institutions offering pre-service, and in-service teacher training; 2) develop a standards and assessment agency, to define performance standards, and assess those standards, through data processing on learning levels at primary, and secondary schools in the country, and promote compatibility with the rest of Europe; 3) finance the development of an education management information system, to enable improved management, and compatibility of information sector wise, among the county's decentralized education systems. It will further test the viability for a budgeting model, designed to promote increased efficiency in education spending; 3) stimulate coordination, innovation, and strategic planning for higher education, through the provision of technical assistance, and training to academics in the country; and, 4) provide reliable data for creating a Poverty Reduction Strategy, and carry out a Living Standards Measurement Study.
