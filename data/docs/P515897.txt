Projet : Central Asia Water Efficiency Cooperation Project
Identifiant : P515897
Pays : Central Asia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 20.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 60 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To enable climate-resilient investments and strengthen the capacity of regional institutions to manage water resources in Central Asia.

Résumé :
The Water Efficiency and Conservation MPA envisages a regional cooperation project, aimed at addressing regional challenges that cannot be effectively resolved through national project financing. An important objective of the regional project is to enable climate-resilient investments and strengthen the capacity of regional institutions to manage water resources in Central Asia.Component 1. Preparation of transboundary projects: Supports preparation of pre-feasibility studies, technical documentation, environmental and social assessments for priority transboundary investments agreed by at least two countries. Focus areas include irrigation and drainage modernization, water conservation, storage, and joint management of shared water systems. Component 2. Information support and digitalization: Finances equipment, technical assistance, and digital solutions to strengthen regional water information systems (ICWC, BWOs Amudarya and Syrdarya). Supports installation of measurement infrastructure, improved data management, and development of analytical tools for water accounting and basin management. Digital innovation, including integration of in-situ and remote sensing data, will enhance forecasting, decision-making, transparency, and regional cooperation, improving water use efficiency and climate resilience.Component 3. Institutional strengthening and capacity building: Strengthens regional governance, institutional capacity, and knowledge exchange to support coordinated, climate-resilient water management.3.1 Legal and strategic frameworks: Harmonization of regional policies and regulations (agreed by participating countries) to support water efficiency, digitalization, and integration of environmental and social considerations.3.2 Institutional capacity: Technical assistance to strengthen IFAS institutions (including ICWC, SIC, BWOs), covering operational capacity, climate-informed decision-making, and implementation of regional reforms.3.3 Knowledge and research: Study tours, annual research and practice conferences, and a regional grants program supporting joint applied research across countries to promote innovation, climate resilience, and regional cooperation.Component 4. Project Management: Supports project implementation, coordination, fiduciary management, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, and compliance through a dedicated PMU, including audits and supervision.
