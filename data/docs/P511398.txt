Projet : Tajikistan Women's Economic Empowerment Project
Identifiant : P511398
Pays : Tajikistan (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 4.73 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 36 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective is to expand women's access to entrepreneurship and jobs opportunities in Tajikistan, with a preference for non-traditional economic sectors.

Résumé :
The project has three components. component 1: Support to individuals and groups on livelihoodscomponent 2: Support to registered women-led or -managed micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) component 3: Project Management, Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E), and communication
