Projet : CN-Nat Hwy4/Hubei-Hunan
Identifiant : P041268
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 952.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 90 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The aim of the proposed Fourth National Highway (NH4) Project is to increase economic activity along the Wuhan-Changsha high-priority transport corridor through relieving congestion, facilitating trade and mobility and helping increase efficiency and traffic safety in Northern Hunan and Southern Hubei.The project development objectives are as follows:(1) Relieve traffic congestion and improve the mobility and integration of inter-provincial trade and commerce between Hunan and Hubei Provinces by assisting in the development of a key section of China's National Trunk Highway System (NTHS) in the principal Beijing-Zhuhai corridor;(2) Strengthen highway institutional capacity at the Hunan and Hubei Provincial Communications Departments (HPCD) and related sector institutions, through policy and institutional reform, provision of training, technical assistance and equipment, in planning, finance, design, construction, operation and maintenance of highway networks;(3) Develop and sustain a policy dialogue in the two key policy areas of the commercialization and corporatization of provincial toll road authorities and of province-wide highway maintenance management; and(4) Improve the safety of road transport.

Résumé :
The Fourth National Highway Project for China aims to increase economic activity along the Wuhan-Changsha high-priority transport corridor through relieving congestion, facilitating trade and mobility, and increasing efficiency and traffic safety in Northern Hunan and Southern Hubei. There are four project components. The first has several subcomponents including construction of two expressway sections; building of interconnecting and other roads; supplying equipment to control construction quality and environmental monitoring, to operate and monitor the new expressway sections, and maintain the provincial road network; and construction supervision services. The second component funds studies and technical assistance in highway maintenance management, transport and trade integration, expressway commercialization and corporatization, and road safety as well as staff training programs covering all aspects of highway planning, design, construction, operation, finance, and maintenance. The third component funds the front-end fee while the fourth component finances land acquisition and resettlement.
