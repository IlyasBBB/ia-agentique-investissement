Projet : Supporting Lebanon's COVID-19 Response for Refugees and Host Communities
Identifiant : P176778
Pays : Lebanon (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 3.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 22 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
10. The project development objective is to support COVID-19 vaccine registration and deployment, as well as COVID-19 response for refugee populations and their host communities in Lebanon.

Résumé :
This RETF will finance 2 components - Component 1: Vaccine registration and deployment. Support of the rollout of the COVID-19 vaccination in areas with a high concentration of refugees and host communities. It includes 2 sub-components: 1.1: Vaccine registration: activities to improve the registration of refugee and host community populations to receive the COVID-19 vaccine; 1.2: Vaccine deployment: activities to improve access and coverage of COVID-19 vaccination for target population through vaccination sites, procurement of supplies needed for vaccination (excluding vaccines), and management of adverse events.- Component 2: COVID-19 response: improved case detection and case management for the refugee population and host communities.
