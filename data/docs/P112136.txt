Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed additional grant would help finance the costs associated with an additional intervention that will provide nutritional supplements and nutrition education to pregnant and lactating women, infants and small children. This is a scaled-up activity of Component C to enhance the impact of the well-performing Community and Basic Health Project.
