Projet : Reproductive and Child Health Project - Phase 2
Identifiant : P110535
Pays : Sierra Leone (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 20.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 83 mois
Année d'approbation : 2010
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase utilization of a package of essential health services by pregnant and lactating women and children under the age of five and support the emergency response needed to contain and control the Ebola crisis.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Second Phase of the Reproductive and Child Health Project for Sierra Leone is to increase utilization of a package of essential health services by pregnant and lactating women and children under the age of five and support the emergency response needed to contain and control the Ebola crisis. The project comprises of two components. The first component, strengthening of service delivery consists of following sub-components: (i) grants to local councils; and (ii) local council grant administration. The second component, capacity building consists of following sub-components: (i) supervision, monitoring and evaluation (M and E); and (ii) medical training.
