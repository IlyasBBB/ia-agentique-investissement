Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROPOSED SAL OPERATION WILL SEEK TO ARTICULATE A MACROECONOMICALLY CONSISTENT POLICY PACKAGE TO: (I) ADDRESS THE DOMESTIC BANKING SYSTEM'S OPERATIONAL AND SOLVENCY PROBLEMS; AND (II) REDUCE THE MAIN REAL-SIDE SECTORAL IMBALANCES THAT CONTRIBUTE TO THOSE PROBLEMS (SPECIFICALLY, IN THE ENERGY, AGRICULTURE AND SOCIAL INSURANCE SECTORS).
