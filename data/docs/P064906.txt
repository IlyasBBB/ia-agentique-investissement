Projet : Poverty Reduction and Local Development Project
Identifiant : P064906
Pays : Nicaragua (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 136.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 68 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The p roject assists the Government of Nicaragua in implementing a portion of its poverty reduction strategy through the Emergency Social Investment Fund (FISE) by: (i) continuing to improve access to priority small-scale social and economic infrastructure and services to the poor; (ii) strengthening local capacity (municipalities, community organizations, NGOs and private sector); and (iii) developing a coherent social safety net. Overall, the project aims at assisting FISE move from a strong focus on infrastructure to a more comprehensive approach to poverty reduction, including greater participation at the local government and community level, as well as contributing to improving the country's safety net.

Résumé :
The Poverty Reduction and Local Development Project, will assist the Government of Nicaragua by partially implementing the poverty reduction strategy, and by continuing to improve access to small-scale social, and economic infrastructure to the poor. The components include: 1) subproject financing, to enable a continued support from the Emergency Social Investment Fund (FISE), in the replacement, expansion, and improvement of basic social services. Training in community organization, operations, and maintenance will be provided. The component aims at improving the quality, and comprehensiveness of investments; ensuring community participation; coordinating with service providers to ensure adequate supply-side interventions; and, addressing the specific needs of indigenous populations; 2) financing of participatory municipal planning, which includes technical assistance (TA), and training provision for communities, and municipalities alike, so as to be able to conduct future planning by themselves. Decentralization of subproject management will continue, through TA from FISE, while capacity building will be provided not only to communities, and municipalities, but to private stakeholders; 3) supporting the development of safety nets within a strengthened social protection strategy, and improved management of public investments in social areas, including monitoring and evaluation of safety net programs. An innovation fund will be established within FISE to pilot safety net measures for poverty reduction; and, 4) project management covering institutional strengthening, supply of equipment, and external auditing.
