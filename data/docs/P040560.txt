Projet : Financial Sector Structural Adjustment Loan
Identifiant : P040560
Pays : Ukraine (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 300.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 29 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT IS DESIGNED TO REFORM AND REINFORCE THE REGULATORY AND LEGAL FRAMEWORK FOR BANKS AND ASSIST IN THE RESTRUCTURING OF ONE OR MORE LARGE BANKS. In December 1999, a loan extension was granted. A further extension of restructuring is under consideration.
