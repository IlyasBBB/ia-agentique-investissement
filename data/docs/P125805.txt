Projet : Port au Prince Neighborhood Housing Reconstruction
Identifiant : P125805
Pays : Haiti (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 65.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 68 mois
Année d'approbation : 2011
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to help residents of Selected Port-au-Prince Neighborhoods severely affected by the Earthquakereturn to their communities by supporting them to repair and/or reconstruct their houses and/or return to improved housingconditions and improving basic community service infrastructure.
