Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to assist Turkey implement a greenhouse gas Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) pilot in the electricity sector, based on Turkey&rsquo;s MRV regulation, and to provide analytical information for the establishment of a carbon market in Turkey.
