Projet : EXPORT DEVELOPMENT 2 ADDITIONAL FINANCING
Identifiant : P109649
Pays : Ukraine (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 150.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 40 mois
Année d'approbation : 2011
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
(i) Serve as a catalyst to support export and real sector growth in Ukraine during the EDP 2 implementation period (2006-2011) and beyond, by providing medium and long-term working capital and investment finance to Ukrainian private exporting enterprises. (ii) Further improve the ability of the Ukrainian banking sector to provide financial resources to the enterprise sector, through further development of intermediation, by expanding its depth and breadth through more and better lending products.
