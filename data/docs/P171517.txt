Projet : Digital Republic of the Marshall Islands Project
Identifiant : P171517
Pays : Marshall Islands (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Numérique
Coût total : 37.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 84 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To expand access to the internet, promote efficient investment in climate resilient digital infrastructure, establish a secure Government network, and the critical foundations for digital government services and the digital economy in the Recipient's territory

Résumé :
The Project will support investments to increase access to digital services. The Project will also support Government’s efforts to move towards an integrated Digital Government Platform that will help to provide greater efficiency and resource-sharing within Government, facilitate better communication with citizens and residents, and introduce a more service-oriented approach to serving individuals and business users. Technical assistance will also be provided on legal, regulatory and policy related issues to build trust around the use of digital services.
