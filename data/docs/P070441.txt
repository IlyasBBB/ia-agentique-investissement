Projet : Hubei Xiaogan-Xiangfan Highway Project
Identifiant : P070441
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 690.88 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 63 mois
Année d'approbation : 2002
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of Hubei Xiaogan-Xiangfan Highway Project is to provide a more effective, safe and efficiently managed transport infrastructure to sustain and support socio-economic development, trade, and regional integration in Hubei province and improve transport links to western lagging regions.The project, when completed, will offer the following socio-economic benefits to Hubei province and other areas influenced by the project:*,,Increased transportation capacity and the consequent decrease in traffic congestion in the Xiaogan-Xiangfan corridor and regional and economic integration with the less developed western areas of Hubei province through the construction of Xiaogan-Xiangfan Expressway.*,,Improved accessibility in low income areas in Hubei province through a program of rehabilitation and upgrading of rural roads in poor counties.*,,Improved management and maintenance of roads and better road safety conditions through the provision of training, technical assistance and equipment to Hubei Provincial Communication Department (HPCD) and related provincial road agencies.

Résumé :
The Hubei Xiaogan - Xiangfan Highway Project will provide effective, safe, and efficiently managed transport infrastructure, to sustain socioeconomic development, trade, and regional integration in Hubei Province, as well as improve transport links to lagging western provinces. The project components will: 1) increase highway capacity, and regional integration, through capital investments in the construction of the Xiaogan - Xiangfan Expressway (XXE). It will link major industrial, and hub cities, and reach less developed counties in the western, and mountainous part of Hubei, stimulating socioeconomic development; 2) include a rural road improvement program, to facilitate accessibility in low-income areas. This component comprises various subprojects for the rehabilitation, and upgrade of existing road sections. The road selection criteria include county income levels, expected impacts on the population served, consistency with provincial priorities, and, economic rate of return; and, 3) strengthen the capacity, and sustainability of highway management in the Province, by building sector institutions, and facilitating institutional transition to the needs of a market economy. To this end, training programs will be provided for government agencies on highway planning, design, supervision, management and operation, and road safety.
