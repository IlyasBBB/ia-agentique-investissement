Projet : MG-Microfinance
Identifiant : P052186
Pays : Madagascar (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 20.4 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 139 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The stated objectives of the project are to increase financial services to low-income populations who do not have access to commercial bank finance by encouraging the government to adopt a policy framework that is appropriate for microfinance institutions (MFIs) and to build the capacity of MFIs, increasing their outreach and sustainability. This first phase of a 15-year Adaptable Program Loan (APL)microfinance program will support achievement of these objectives through the implementation of four components:1. Development of an appropriate legal/regulatory and supervisory framework for microfinance institutions;2. Establishment of MFIs and in particular savings and loan associations (SLAs) in four of Madagascar's six provinces (Antananarivo, Antsiranana, Fianarantsoa, and Toamasina). If studies confirm their viability, programs will be started in the two remaining provinces (Mahajanga and Toliary).3. Design and testing of a pilot national training program;4. Undertaking of studies related to microfinance to expand knowledge in the field.

Résumé :
The Microfinance Project aims at providing increased financial services to the low-income population not served by the traditional banking sector. The first phase of the microfinance program will encourage the government to adopt a policy framework that is appropriate to microfinance institutions (MFIs) and to build the capacity of MFIs in increasing their outreach and sustainability, including their capacity to mobilize financial resources. There are four components in the program: 1) The program will improve legal regulatory and judicial and supervisory framework 2) The program will develop 47 existing Savings and Loan Associations and create 202 new ones, including monitor them closely. 3) The program will develop training program and build capabilities in microfinance. One of the objectives is to develop in Madagascar both a market and a professional expertise in the field of microfinance training, and to build a sustainable training mechanism for those who will manage the financial institutions and those who will supervise them. 4) The program will expand knowledge in microfinance by supporting a number of specific audits and studies.
