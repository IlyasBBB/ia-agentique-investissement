Projet : Strengthening Early Childhood Development and Basic Education Systems to Support Human Capital Development in Eswatini Project
Identifiant : P173151
Pays : Eswatini (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 32.1 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 74 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To strengthen education service delivery and management systems in the early years and junior secondary education

Résumé :
In order to improve human capital development outcomes, the project takes a life-cycle approach using education as an entry point, with a focus on two areas of intervention: Early Childhood Care, Development and Education (ECCDE) and Basic Education. Four components are supported under the project.Component 1 aims to improve access to quality ECCDE services, including by strengthening coordination and regulation of the sub-sector. The component supports interventions that aim to improve coherence in the management of ECCDE services by undertaking mapping of ECCDE service providers and conducting regular quality assurance assessments. In addition, the project supports the improvement of the quality of ECCDE services by developing a comprehensive package for Grade 0, encompassing learning materials, curriculum, teacher guides, indoor and outdoor learning resources, and teacher training, and rolling it out in selected ECCDE service providers.Component 2 focuses on improving quality and internal efficiency in Basic Education by supporting literacy and numeracy interventions in the early primary grades and integrating Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in mathematics and science education in junior secondary grades. The component also supports the implementation of the newly adopted Competency Based Education (CBE) curriculum and institutionalizes technology-enabled instruction in mathematics and science in the Teacher Training Faculty of the University of Eswatini and the William Pitcher College of Education. To improve retention in secondary education, the component addresses both demand-side and supply-side constraints faced by Orphans and Vulnerable Children (OVCs), with a particular focus on strengthening the OVC grant.Component 3 strengthens key management capacities of the Ministry of Education and Training, regional and local education offices, and supports the Project Support Unit, which plays a key role in the coordination and management of the project, including procurement, financial management, monitoring and evaluation, and environmental and social safeguards.Component 4 is a Contingent Emergency Response Component to enable timely response to crises that may impact the education sector.
