Projet : Cubango-Okavango River Basin (CORB): Financing Innovation in Transboundary Waters
Identifiant : P513349
Pays : Southern Africa (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 10.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to support the capitalization of the Cubango-Okavango River Basin Fund (“CORB Fund”) towards sustainable development in the Cubango-Okavango river basin (“CORB”).

Résumé :
The Cubango-Okavango river basin (CORB) is a hydrologically complex, and deeply interconnected system which highlights the importance of transboundary solutions. Community livelihoods, ranging from artisanal fisheries to small-scale agriculture in all three riparian states (Angola, Namibia and Botswana) depend on the CORB’s floodplains, rivers, wetlands and other natural resources. These resources are also vital for larger industry, including tourism. Water resources in the CORB are facing competing demands that jeopardize the sustainability of livelihoods and ecosystems. In addition to the threats posed by poverty, rapid rural population growth, and climate change, the CORB’s water resources face demands from urbanization trends; resource-based livelihoods; expanding tourism; commercial agriculture; and water extraction. Addressing these challenges require innovations in transboundary water financing to achieve economies of scale and scope for transboundary basin financing needs that cannot otherwise be accomplished at a national level. The objective of the Project is to support the capitalization of the CORB Fund towards sustainable development in the Cubango-Okavango river basin. The operationalization of the CORB Fund will be instrumental to enable a regional funding mechanism for cross-border financing needs with strong governance and decision-making capabilities, and with capacity to raise and channel financing from a diverse set of stakeholders. Project support includes the preparation of viable project concepts on the CORB Fund pillars, as well as strengthening the coordination, management, and communication between all the stakeholders involved in the basin, thus enhancing strategies to raise additional funding.Component 1 focuses on the CORB Fund’s capitalization. This component will support the consolidation and expansion of the CORB Fund as an innovative financial mechanism, and advance collaboration towards its long-term sustainability. It includes (1.1.) the establishment and implementation of the CORB Fund’s governance, operational tools, and systems, (1.2.) the implementation of stakeholder coordination and engagement for long-term sustainability of the CORB Fund, (1.3.) support to the CORB Fund's communication and knowledge management, and (1.4.) CORB endowment fund capitalization. Component 2 focuses on developing the CORB Fund’s pipeline for potential investment. This component will focus on the development of the CORB Fund’s pipeline of investment to address the priority areas identified by Angola, Botswana and Namibia in the CORB, laying the foundation for larger/more complex investments in the future. The component’s activities will focus on two complementary areas of support: (2.1) preparing the CORB Fund Financing Roadmap as well as project concepts at pre-feasibility and feasibility level for potential investment; and (2.2) enhancing capacity on economics of biodiversity to inform investment. The development of the pipeline for potential future investment will be conducted with close engagement with the Ministers of Finance of Riparian countries to ensure high level buy-in and engagement participation in the preparation of the Fund’s long-term capitalization and development into the role as a regional financial institution. Component 3 focuses on project management, monitoring and evaluation (M&E).
