Projet : Social Fund Project
Identifiant : P044824
Pays : Comoros (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 13.7 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 79 mois
Année d'approbation : 1997
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT WILL CONTINUE TO FINANCE FURTHER ACTIVITIES UNDER THEON-GOING SOCIAL FUND. THIS IS A FOLLOW-UP PROJECT.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Social Fund Project is to address the incidence of poverty amongst the country's poorer communities through the replenishment of a social fund designed to support demand-driven initiatives developed by such communities and, in doing so, to create employment and improve access to basic social services. The project builds on the very successful social fund component of the on-going Population and Human Resources Project. The Social Fund Project components are: 1) rehabilitation/construction of communal infrastructure for primary schools, health centers, feeder roads, community water supply systems, markets, etc.; 2) micro-credit activities, such as, subproject financing for income generating activities and small loan programs for individuals, as well as groups, particularly women; 3) capacity building subprojects for training of Fonds d'Appui au Developpement Communautaire (FADC)'s partners to improve their effectiveness in identifying, preparing, and implementing community development activities; and 4) training and institution capacity building technical assistance for FADC's investment and operating costs, audits, and studies.
