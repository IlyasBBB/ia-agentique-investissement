Projet : South Sudan Resilient Agricultural Livelihoods Project
Identifiant : P169120
Pays : South Sudan (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 62.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is to strengthen capacity of farmers and their organizations and improve agricultural production.

Résumé :
The Project will scale up support to vulnerable households through expanded food and nutrition assistance while strengthening food security crisis preparedness and resilience. Building on previous World Bank investments and complementing other development partner programs, the Project will continue to support increased agricultural production, productivity, and market participation through the provision of productive inputs, improved seeds, farm tools, extension and advisory services, skills development, and strengthened seed systems. The Project will also support the establishment of foundational systems for strategic food reserve management and emergency response, contributing to enhanced resilience to food security shocks and climate-related risks.
