Projet : ID-KECAMATAN DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM Supplem
Identifiant : P072355
Pays : Indonesia (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 48.2 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 24 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To provide 3rd year support through Supplement Credit to the Kecamatan Development Credit. Objectives are development of community-based planning and provision of physical and economic infrastructure for 750 poor Kecamatans in 19 provinces of Indonesia. Means are through unallocated block grants given to subdistricts and private sector/NGO facilitation TA
