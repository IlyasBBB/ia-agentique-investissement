Projet : Additional Financing for Social Support for Resilient Livelihoods Project
Identifiant : P177813
Pays : Malawi (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 187.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 67 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the Project are to improve resilience among the poor and vulnerable population and to strengthen the national platform for safety nets in the Republic of Malawi.

Résumé :
The proposed Additional Financing (AF) will continue supporting the government led second Malawi National Social Support Program (MNSSP II), by expanding and rationalizing support to the social protection system in Malawi. In this context, the AF will contribute to the MNSSP II in two ways, as reflected in the PDO. The first part of the PDO highlights the importance of improving resilience, which is understood as the ability of households to improve initial levels of welfare in the face of chronic stress and to maintain welfare in the face of shocks. In this regard the AF emphasizes rationalization and scaling up of core safety net programs which boost economic and social inclusion. The resilience of these programs will be further strengthened by the delivery systems and capacity investments under the project. The second part of the PDO highlights the need to more strongly support the effectiveness of a national system for social safety nets under the MNSSP II framework. The AF places emphasis on this through efforts to strengthen harmonized delivery systems (including social registry, e-payments and citizens engagement), as well as efforts to strengthen overall capacity and institutional strengthening in the sector. The AF strengthens support across all four major components in the original project: (i) Component 1: Improving Social and Economic Inclusion. This component focuses on the consolidation of an appropriate package of support for beneficiary households with a mix of program interventions, including Social Cash Transfers Program (SCTP), livelihoods support, and Enhanced climate smart Public Works Program (EPWP), as well as an option for scalable financing in time of crisis. The AF supports this component through filling an original financing gap on Social Cash Transfer Programme (SCTP); scaling up climate-smart Enhanced Public Works Program (EPWP) including for resource poor households exiting the Affordable Input Program (AIP); scaling up both the livelihood support activities and option for scalable financing in time of crisis; (ii) Component 2: Strengthening Harmonized Delivery Systems the AF will focus on strengthening social registry and information management, introduction of e-payments and citizens engagement; (iii) Component 3: Capacity Building and Institutional Strengthening Support to support the setting up of the necessary institutional structure for AF implementation, capacity building and general project management and evaluation and (iv) Component 4: Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC) a grant from the Crisis Response Window (CRW) Early Response Fund (ERF) will pre-allocate US$12.5 million equivalent for supporting social protection based response in the event of a disaster or crisis that has caused or is likely to imminently cause a major food security crisis.
