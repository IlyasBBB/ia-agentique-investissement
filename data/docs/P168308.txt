Projet : Urban Resilience and Solid Waste Management Project
Identifiant : P168308
Pays : Cote d'Ivoire (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 315.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 85 mois
Année d'approbation : 2020
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The development objective of the project is to reduce vulnerability to flooding in selected urban areas.

Résumé :
The proposed restructuring will change the structure of component 2 : Improvement of solid waste management infrastructure and services. The proposed project components structure is: 1) Flood risk mitigation infrastructure and services: i) urban drainage and associated roadworks; ii) nature-based solutions for erosion and landslide control; iii) emergency preparedness ad early warning system and iv) resilient urban planning;2) Improvement of solid waste management services through institutional strengthening: i) Strengthening sector governance, institutional capacity and citizen engagement and ii) improving solid waste management through citizen engagement, recycling, reuse, composting and digital technology 3) Project management support: technical assistance, equipment, training and operating costs for the Project Coordination Unit and Specialized Implementation Agencies.4) Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC).
