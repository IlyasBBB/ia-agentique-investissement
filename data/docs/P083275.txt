Projet : Niger Public Expenditure Reform Credit
Identifiant : P083275
Pays : Niger (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 40.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 10 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed credit is designed to help implement the Government's economic reform program during 2005-2006 and Niger's PRSP by supporting key policy measures aiming at: (i) deepening the reforms in public expenditure management; (ii) increasing the impact on the poor of public spending in key priority areas, including public service delivery in education and health, and investments for agricultural growth and rural development; and (iii) improving the monitoring and evaluation system for the implementation of the PRSP and its revision.

Résumé :
The proposed credit for the Public Expenditure Reform for Niger is designed to help implement the Government's economic reform program during 2005-2006 and Niger's Poverty Reduction Strategy Paper (PRSP) by supporting key policy measures aiming at: (i) deepening the reforms in public expenditure management; (ii) increasing the impact on the poor of public spending in key priority areas, including public service delivery in education and health, and investments for agricultural growth and rural development; and (iii) improving the monitoring and evaluation system for the implementation of the PRSP and its revision.
