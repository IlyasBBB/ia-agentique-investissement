Projet : Mozambique: Communication Sector Reform Project
Identifiant : P073479
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Numérique
Coût total : 15.4 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 91 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Improve access to and quality of efficient and affordable communications services by creating an enabling environment for competition and private participation in sectors deemed critical to facilitate national and regional market integration, i.e., telecommunications, postal and air transport infrastructure and services.

Résumé :
The Communication Sector Reform Project will improve the access, and quality of communications services in Mozambique, by creating a competitive environment, with private participation in the telecommunications, postal, and air transport infrastructure, and services. Project components will: 1) develop a telecommunication policy, through consulting services to reflect the principles of liberalization, independent regulation, and privatization of the Public Telecommunication Company (TDM). Assistance will also be provided to the Ministry of Transport and Communications in building capacity in sector analysis, policy formulation, and monitoring criteria to liaise throughout the reform process. Additionally, a comprehensive telecommunications, and postal legal, and regulatory framework will be prepared, by strengthening the Telecommunications and Postal Regulatory Authority's capacity, and, technical assistance (TA) will support TDM's privatization. A strategy for market-based rural access should establish provision of seed money, and universal fund access, while a modernization strategy will elaborate a postal policy focused on competitiveness to improve services, in addition to enabling legislation for an information, and communications (IC) technology strategy; 2) develop an air transport policy, and regulatory framework, by strengthening the capacity of the Civil Aviation Policy Unit, through the provision of TA, training, and consulting services, to restructure, and foster private participation of the national airline, and to implement the concession of the Maputo International Airport; and, 3) support project management, procurement, and financial management, and, finance IC campaign and dissemination.
