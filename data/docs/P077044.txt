Identifiant : P077044
Pays : Brazil (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 1.65 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 37 mois
Année d'approbation : 2002
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The development objective of the proposed project is to strengthen the Institutional and Operational capacity of the Amazon Working Group (GTA) to efficiently represent the Amazon civil society in the Pilot Program to Conserve the Brazilian Rain Forest (RFPP). GTA is un mbrella non-governmental organization for Brazilian NGOs in the Amazon region, formed by the coalition of some 500 grassroots organizations in the Amazon. The project will support the improvement of GTA management capacity, strategic planing, fund-raising and communication.

Résumé :
The Brazil Amazon Working Group (GTA) Institutional Strengthening Project aims to strengthen the institutional capacity of the Amazon Working Group (GTA) to efficiently represent the Amazon civil society in the Pilot Program to Conserve the Brazilian Rain Forest (RFPP). The Amazon Working Group is a coalition of 500 grassroots Amazonian Non-governmental organizations (NGOs). The project includes the following components: improvement of GTA technical and operational capacity; improvement of GTA fund raising and financial sustainability; improvement of GTA internal and External Communication; and improvement of GTA institutional capacity to represent Amazon NGOs.
