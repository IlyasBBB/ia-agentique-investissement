Projet : Angola Youth Employment Opportunities Project
Identifiant : P505158
Pays : Angola (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 250.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 52 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase productivity and access to employment opportunities among targeted youth in Angola.

Résumé :
The Project aims to increase productivity and access to employment opportunities among targeted youth, thereby contributing to Angola's economic transformation and diversification efforts. Building on global evidence, the project will employ an integrated approach (with a set of differentiated interventions) to address the diverse constraints faced by youth. The integrated approach will combine supply and demand-side interventions, as well as employment and intermediation services, with a view to leverage the comprehensive approach required to address the youth employment challenge for Angola’s economic transformation.
