Projet : Skills for Employment and Economic Transformation in Mozambique
Identifiant : P513981
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 238.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 62 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Operation is to create more and better jobs in priority value chains by strengthening the skills ecosystem.

Résumé :
MozJobs aims to create more and better jobs in priority value chains by strengthening the skills ecosystem (priority sectors are energy, agribusinesses, and tourism). It does this by making the skills system industry-led and data-driven: rolling out a national TVET-MIS, strengthening the LMIS with predictive analytics, and operationalizing institute fee retention so providers are incentivized to perform. On the delivery side, MozJobs finances competitive, employer-linked grants, work-based internships, and modernized qualifications and short-cycle programs—including hybrid/remote learning backed by LMS analytics. It also scales collaborative short courses in priority value chains, entrepreneurship and socio-emotional skills, and Recognition of Prior Learning; and, through two African Centers of Excellence, boosts higher-education quality, research, and innovation.
