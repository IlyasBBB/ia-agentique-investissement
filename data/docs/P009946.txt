Projet : NAT. HIGHWAYS II
Identifiant : P009946
Pays : India (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 385.3 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 128 mois
Année d'approbation : 1992
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
TO MODERNIZE (REHAB, STRENGTHEN, WIDEN) ABOUT 290 KM OF NAT'L H'WAYSIN 6 STATES & TO PROMOTE IMPROVEMENTS IN ROAD ENG'G & CONSTRUCTION,NETWORK MGMNT & CONTRACT MGMNT. GOI FEELS THAT NEGOTIATIONS SHOULDBE POSTPONED TO AFTER THE CREATION OF THE NEW HIGH POWER COMMITTEEIS SUBMITTED TO THE NEW GOVT.

Résumé :
The objectives of the project are to: (i) modernize key sections of the national highway network; (ii) promote improvements in: road engineering and construction; network management; and contract management; and (iii) reconstruct selected flood damaged bridges in the State of Orissa. The project includes: (a) modernization of six sections, about 290 kilometers, of heavily trafficked and congested national highways in Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Orissa, Punjab and West Bengal; (b) equipment for monitoring and management of the national highway network; (c) institutional strengthening including: (i) training of the Ministry of Surface Transport (MOST) and the Public Works Department staff for works supervision; (ii) technical assistance to MOST; and (d) studies to prepare follow-up projects. Reconstruction of six bridges in Ganjam District in Orissa is also included in this project.
