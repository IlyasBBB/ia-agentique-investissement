Projet : Angola Digital Acceleration Project
Identifiant : P180693
Pays : Angola (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Numérique
Coût total : 300.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 2024
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To accelerate digital inclusion, increase access to digitally enabled services, and unleash digital opportunities for the advancement of Angola’s digital economy.

Résumé :
The proposed project is designed to support the Government of Angola unlock digital acceleration through four mutually reinforcing components around digital inclusion, the enabling environment for digital public service delivery, and development of a trust environment. The fourth component, project management, builds the capacity of the country’s digital transformation agency, Instituto de Modernização Administrativa (IMA), to lead and support coordination of the country’s digital acceleration agenda, thereby ensuring greater impact and sustainability of the interventions and reforms under the first three components.
