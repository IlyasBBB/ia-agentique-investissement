Projet : CN THIRD ODS PHASE OUT
Identifiant : P003409
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 120.1 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 162 mois
Année d'approbation : 1995
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
<H>The key development objective</> of this Montreal Protocol Project is the phase-out of Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS). Through a combination of regulatory measures, public information and financial assistance to enterprises, China has developed a large ODS phaseout program.<H>The global development objective</> of fully reversing the damage to the Ozone layer will only be met as a result of all global actions taken from 1987 to the present, and much of it has already been taken in the industrial countries. Even so, this process will take sixty to seventy years to complete. There is reason to assume that the global development objective will be met, even though it is in the very distant future. China represents one of the largest remaining ODS producers and consumers. Completed subprojects in the ODS III Project have achieved their targeted phaseout. The remaining subprojects under implementation are expected to achieve their target phaseout as well.
