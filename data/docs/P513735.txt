Projet : Mali Emergency Access to Essential Services Project
Identifiant : P513735
Pays : Mali (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 150.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 13 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To maintain continued access to essential health and education services in Mali.

Résumé :
The operation will help to ensure the continued delivery of essential services during a period of exceptional fiscal stress. It aims to keep critical health services available and safe by financing vaccines and essential medications (including vaccines/drugs against malaria, tuberculosis, and Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome, as well as allocating resources to national and regional hospitals and specialized health agencies. Education support will be provided in the form of (i) tuition fee waivers for lower and upper secondary students, and (ii) financing the logistics, procurement, printing and distribution of national examinations.
