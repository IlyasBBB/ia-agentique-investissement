Projet : Additional Financing on Vaccines for the Liberia COVID-19 Emergency Response Project
Identifiant : P176336
Pays : Liberia (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 8.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 39 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The development objective is to prepare and respond to the COVID-19 pandemic in Liberia

Résumé :
The Liberia COVID-19 response project aims to strengthen the Government of Liberia’s immediate capacity to respond to the COVID-19 outbreak and in the longer-term strengthen its capacity to respond to disease outbreaks and emergencies. The project will have fivecomponents: Emergency Preparedness and Response , Laboratory system strengthening, Case management and clinical care , Risk communication, community engagement and advocacy and Project management and coordination including monitoring and evaluation.
