Projet : Senegal:AG.EXPORT PROMOTION
Identifiant : P051610
Pays : Senegal (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 10.4 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 79 mois
Année d'approbation : 1997
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT'S MAIN OBJECTIVES ARE TO HELP EXPAND AND DIVERSIFY THE AG. EXPORT BASE OF SENEGAL THROUGH THE PROVISION OF EXPORT PROMOTION AND DIVERSIFICATION SUPPORT; STRENGHTENING OF PRODUCERS' AND EXPORTERS' ORGANIZATIONS; UPGRADING EXPORT INFRASTRUCTURE; AND SUPPORT TO PROJECT MANAGEMENT.

Résumé :
The objectives of the Agricultural Export Promotion Project are to diversify and develop private agricultural exports, a core objective of Senegal's export-led growth strategy. The project has three components: 1) export promotion and diversification activities including a) assisting Senegalese producers/exporters in market research, customer identification, meeting quality requirements, trial shipments, obtaining pre-export finance and developing maritime agricultural export crops; b) product development and processing, and quality control via targeted adaptive research; and c) establishment of business partnerships between local exporters and foreign businesses, and assistance in identifying regional and overseas niches; 2) strengthening of existing private producers/exporters' professional organizations, encouraging the creation of farmers' associations where needed, and setting up a central referral/data information service as well as providing management and legal advice; and 3) upgrading of export facilities at the airport of Dakar to preserve perishable products in transit; and 4) project management.
