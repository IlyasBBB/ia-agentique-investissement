Projet : Uganda: Roads and Bridges in the Refugee Hosting Districts/Koboko-Yumbe-Moyo Road Corridor Project
Identifiant : P171339
Pays : Uganda (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 150.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 86 mois
Année d'approbation : 2020
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objectives are to enhance: (a) road transport connectivity in select refugee hosting districts of Uganda; and (b) the capacity of the Ministry of Works and Transport to manage environmental, social and road safety risks.

Résumé :
Uganda is host to the largest number of refugees in Africa and the third largest number of refugees worldwide. For several decades, Uganda has been generously hosting refugees and asylum seekers from many conflict-affected countries in its neighborhood and many refugees, especially in the northern districts, are in protracted displacement. The Government of Uganda is keen to improve transport connectivity in the refugee hosting areas to enhance access, facilitate delivery of supplies, and benefit both refugees and host communities.The Project seeks to improve transport connectivity in the refugee hosting districts in the northern region and facilitate supplies and services to the refugees by upgrading the 105 km-long Koboko-Yumbe-Moyo (KYM) road corridor in the West Nile Sub-region. The project is also aimed at capacity enhancement of the Ministry of Works and Transport to manage environmental, social and road safety risks.
