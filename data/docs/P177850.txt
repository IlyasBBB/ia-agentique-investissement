Projet : Sierra Leone COVID-19 Emergency Preparedness and Response Project Second Additional Financing
Identifiant : P177850
Pays : Sierra Leone (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 18.1 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 19 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To prevent, detect and respond to the threat posed by COVID-19 and strengthen national systems for public health preparedness in Sierra Leone.

Résumé :
The second additional financing (AF2) will support scaling up of the COVID-19 vaccination in Sierra Leone. It will provide upfront financing to help the Government of Sierra Leone (GoSL) to scale up risk communication and community engagement, vaccine acquisitionthrough the African Vaccine Acquisition Trust (AVAT), and its deployment, which meet World Bank’s vaccine approval criteria (VAC) for affordable and equitable access to COVID-19 vaccination in Sierra Leone. The AF2 will also support strengthening diagnostic capacity for early detection and containment of the virus.
