Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THIS PROJECT WOULD PROVIDE TECHNICAL AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE INIMPLEMENTING A RECOVERY STRATEGY OF THE AREA AFFECTED BY THE MASCARAEARTHQUAKE ON 8/13/94. IT WOULD INCLUDE RECONSTRUCTION OF PUBLIC IN-FRASTRUCTURES AND HOUSING DESTROYED BY THE EARTHQUAKE, AND MEASURESPREVENTING HAZARDS FROM SIMILAR DISASTERS IN THE FUTURE.
