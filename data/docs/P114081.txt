Projet : AR AF Buenos Aires Province Infra Sust Investment Development
Identifiant : P114081
Pays : Argentina (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 66.67 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 58 mois
Année d'approbation : 2010
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The development objectives (PDOs) of this project are to: (i) enhance the provision of water andsewerage services for the benefitof low-income people, in particular for those peopleliving in highly vulnerable areas; (ii) improve high priority road segments of theBorrower’s road network; (iii) mitigate urban flooding; and (iv) support thereactivation of the Borrower’s economy and strengthen its regional competitiveness.
