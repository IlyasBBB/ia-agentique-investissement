Projet : Emergency Social Protection Project
Identifiant : P099295
Pays : Iraq (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 8.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 83 mois
Année d'approbation : 2006
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The overall objective of the project is to strengthen the policy, management and administrative capacity in MOLSA and MOF to develop, manage and monitor pension and social safety net reforms. Since this is a small Technical Assistance project, which will be implemented over a short period of time, it will be difficult to measure final outcomes. One intermediate measure of success will be that both MOLSA and MOF are able to use information generated by the new systems for social safety net and pension decision making.
