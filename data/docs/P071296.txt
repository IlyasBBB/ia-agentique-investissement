Projet : URBAN SECTOR DEVELOPMENT REFORM PROJECT
Identifiant : P071296
Pays : Indonesia (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 57.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 96 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is to assist the Borrower to strengthen local governance and improve the provision of selected urban services by participating Urban Local Governments (ULGs) and additional participating ULGs.

Résumé :
The Indonesia Urban Sector Development and Reform Project aims to improve urban services in the participating Urban Local Governments (ULG). This is the first step toward achieving the Government of Indonesia's (GOI) long-term goal to develop self-reliant cities. To achieve this objective, the project focuses on reforming municipal governance, developing institutional capacity and financing priority urban investments. There are two project components. A. Urban Reform and Institutional Support Component, supports the ULGs with implementing urban reform programs, that is (a) core governance reforms in the areas of civic participation and transparency, procurement and financial management; (b) long-term urban development strategies and plans, including local economic development and urban poverty reduction strategies; and (c) institutional and capacity development programs. This component will also support the Central Project Management Unit (CPMU) in project management. The second component, Urban Investment, finances prioritized investments for improving urban services in the participating ULGs.
