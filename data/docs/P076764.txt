Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed Structural Adjustment Credit (SAC) would support the governments of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (FRY) and one of its constituent republics, the Republic of Montenegro, in the implementation of a structural reform agenda focused on two broad themes: (i) the sustainable control of fiscal and quasi-fiscal imbalances; and (ii) the promotion of enterprise restructuring and improvement of the business environment. The SAC could focus on reforms in four areas. Two reforming and enhancing the transparency of public expenditure management, and reform of the pension system are particularly crucial for improving fiscal sustainability. Reforms in two other areas labor markets and the business environment while also contributing to fiscal sustainability, are primarily focused on support of enterprise restructuring, creation of a vibrant private sector, and integration of the gray economy into the official economy. Given the quasi-fiscal pressures emerging from the enterprise sector, the latter reforms would also promote fiscal sustainability by limiting the fiscal impacts of such restructuring.
