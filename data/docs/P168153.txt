Projet : Market Access for Agricultural Products Project
Identifiant : P168153
Pays : Paraguay (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 110.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 93 mois
Année d'approbation : 2020
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Enhance access to markets and provide emergency support to Agricultural Producer Organizations, Indigenous Communities and their members, for the current COVID-19 emergency and future Eligible Crises or Emergencies.

Résumé :
The development objective of Market Access for Agricultural Products Project is to enhance access to markets by Agricultural Producer Organizations and Indigenous Communities in selected parts of the Borrower’s territory. This project has three components. 1) The first component, Capacity Building and Institutional Strengthening, has the following subcomponents: (i) Capacity building of Agricultural Producer Organizations and Indigenous Communities; and (ii) Public Sector Institutional Strengthening. 2) The second component, Enhancing Market Access through Productive Alliances, aims is to promote the development of organizations of small and medium‐sized farmers and indigenous communities participating in productive alliances. 3) The third component, Project Coordination, Management and Monitoring and Evaluation, aims to provide support to Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock (MAG) for project coordination, management, monitoring, evaluation and communication.
