Projet : Lebanon Digital Acceleration Project
Identifiant : P506791
Pays : Lebanon (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Numérique
Coût total : 150.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 65 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To strengthen digital foundations and enablers to improve the delivery of high-impact public services.

Résumé :
The proposed operation aims to support the implementation of digital foundations and enablers such as digital public infrastructure (including digital ID, electronic signature, and trusted data sharing platforms), modern data infrastructure and cloud computing, cybersecurity, data protection, artificial intelligence, and digital skills, to improve the delivery of high-impact public services.
