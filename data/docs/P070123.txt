Projet : DZ-Privatization Assistance (LIL)
Identifiant : P070123
Pays : Algeria (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 5.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 42 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
In response to GOA request, a first mission will identify the proposed Privatization LIL in mid-Feburary. The objective of the LIL is to provide policy support in the elaboration of the GOA's privatization program as well as to the build institutional capacity to implement the program. Preparation costs are likely to be high, and preparation possibly lengthy, due to the need to forge a consensus around the to-be-proposed program as well as a complete lack of institutional infrastructure (the Minister of Participation (Privatization) has only been appointed late-December and there exists no administrative base for the Ministry.

Résumé :
The Privatization assistance Project will create the basis for an accelerated privatization program, through a supportive institutional framework. The components will: 1) strengthen the institutional capacity of the Ministry of Participation and Coordination of Economic Reforms (MPCR), financing the resources, and expertise required for policy, and legal advisory services. Additionally, technical advisors will support the development of, and preparation for enterprise privatization. It includes funding for specialists, and study tours for MPCR staff; 2) strengthen the Conseil de Privatisation transaction capacity, through the support of advisory services, and, investment banks will provide assistance in complex transactions. Advisory services will also support the design of specific outreach, and information activities; and, 3) ensure adequate general, and financial management of the project, namely, supporting the project implementation unit with strong procurement, and financial management.
