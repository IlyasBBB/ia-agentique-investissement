Projet : Cameroon-Chad Transport Corridor
Identifiant : P167798
Pays : Western and Central Africa (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 816.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 124 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed project development objective is to improve the efficiency and safety of regional trade transport along the Douala-N’Djamena intermodal corridor.

Résumé :
The development objective of Cameroon-Chad Transport Corridor is to improve the efficiency and safety of regional trade transport along the Douala-N’Djamena intermodal corridor. This project has five components. The first component, Douala-Yaoundé rail track rehabilitation and railway signaling modernization, has the following sub-components: Rail track rehabilitation between Douala and Yaoundé; Signaling system modernization; and Safeguard measures implementation. The second component, Rail/road connections investments, railway capacity building and road safety, has the following sub-components: Improving the efficiency of rail/road modal transfer facilities; Railway sector institutional strengthening; and Road safety capacity building. The third component, Road reconstruction, maintenance and safety improvements, has the following sub-components: N’Djamena-Moundou-Koutéré road corridor rehabilitation and maintenance program; Axle load control stations upgrading; Road safety capacity building activities; and Safeguard measures implementation. The fourth component, Trade facilitation and project implementation support, has the following sub-components: Supporting the modernization of customs administrations in Chad and Cameroon with a view to improving the efficiency of customs operations through the interconnection of customs information systems; and development of the professionalization of customs staff, brokers, and services users; Supporting the modernization of the transport sector and the professionalization of public and private sector actors involved in transit, transport, and logistics operations; Supporting the dematerialization program of Douala Single Window to expand its services across Cameroon; Institutional strengthening and capacity building, supporting bilateral dialogue on trade facilitation; and Project management. The fifth component, Contingent Emergency Response (CERC), will draw from the uncommitted credit resources under the project to cover emergency response.
