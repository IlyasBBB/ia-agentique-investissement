Projet : Emergency Stabilization of Electricity Supply Project (Montenegro)
Identifiant : P077473
Pays : Montenegro (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 7.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 58 mois
Année d'approbation : 2002
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To test whether electricity consumers will reduce illegal use of electricity and increase payment rates in response to the introduction of remote metering, automated billing and demand side management in pilot areas.

Résumé :
The main purpose of the Emergency Stabilization of Electricity Supply in the Republic of Montenegro Project, is to test the viability of, and the degree electric power distribution consumers demonstrate in improving their payment discipline. The Project will also participate in a loss reduction program in response to the introduction of remote metering, automated billing, and demand side management (DSM) in the pilot areas. The learning expectations to be documented through this pilot include the economic, and financial impacts of the distribution program; the distribution of automated technologies; the impact of changes on the institutional capacity of the Electric Power Company of Montenegro (EPCG); and, the social responsiveness to alternative service practices. The two main components will: 1) provide funds for the supply, and installation of equipment required for the provision of remote metering, automated billing, and DSM in selected areas. Technical assistance (TA) will be provided for the design, procurement, training, and implementation of pilot projects, as well as for monitoring and evaluation; and, 2) finance the supply, and installation of a computer-based management information system (MIS) in EPCG, which also includes TA for its design, procurement, training and implementation.
