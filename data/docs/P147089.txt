Projet : Expanding Community Approaches in Conflict Situations in Three Southernmost Provinces in Thailand
Identifiant : P147089
Pays : Thailand (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 4.2 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 52 mois
Année d'approbation : 2013
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is to contribute to confidence-building between communities and sub-district authorities insouthern Thailand through participatory local development approaches in six sub-districts, and through capacity building ofselected local government officials as well as selected civil society networks and organizations.
