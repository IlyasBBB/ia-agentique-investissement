Projet : Enhanced Support to Prioritized Utilities in the Western Balkans
Identifiant : P515685
Pays : Western Balkans (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Numérique
Coût total : 2.74 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 29 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective is to strengthen the capacity of selected water and sanitation utilities in the Western Balkans to improve operational performance and investment readiness through enhanced non-revenue water management, digitalization and asset management practices.

Résumé :
The proposed project focuses primarily on supporting implementation activities for the first five shortlisted utilities, to be executed through the International Association of Water Service Companies in the Danube Region (IAWD) as the implementing agency. Beneficiary utilities will receive support from IAWD to implement the projects identified during the Bank Executed Utility of the Future (UoF) program. Implementation will draw on a combination of technical assistance, consulting and non-consulting services, and procurement of goods.
