Projet : BZ ROADS AND MUNICIPAL DRAINAGE PROJECT
Identifiant : P040150
Pays : Belize (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 18.4 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Board approved the amendment ot the loan agreeement in October 2004 inclusive of the dropping of the objective and associated component (c) in view of its absor ption under a parallel project funded by the IDB.

Résumé :
The Roads and Municipal Drainage Project for Belize aims to a) improve year-round traffic flows on the national road network; b) improve natural drainage and reduce floods and/or the impact of floods in the selected municipalities; c) asses the potential of private sector participation in road maintenance activities; and d) improve the management of the transport sector, including transport policy coordination among the different government ministries and agencies. There are four main project components. The first improves a road section connecting the Northern Highway to the Western Highway. The second component implements road maintenance by contract as part of a pilot project at one or more of seven districts and strengthens the maintenance department of the ministry of works and transport. It also finances technical assistance, vehicles and computer equipment, physical works, and supervision for a pilot project. The third component supports the improvement of the drainage system for selected municipalities. The fourth component finances technical assistance comprising studies, engineering services, and training staff at the Ministry of works and Transport.
