Projet : Guiyang Transport Project
Identifiant : P093963
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 314.42 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 72 mois
Année d'approbation : 2008
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase the access and mobility of Guiyang Municipality's transport users through priority infrastructure investments while establishing more sustainable mechanisms for rural road maintenance, as well as enhancing capacity for keeping municipal debt under control.

Résumé :
The Guiyang Transport Project will assist China in increasing the access and mobility of Guiyang Municipality's transport users through priority infrastructure investments while establishing more sustainable mechanisms for rural road maintenance, as well as enhancing capacity for keeping municipal debt under control. The project includes the following three components: A) Youxiao Road - the construction of an approximately 7 km four-lane to six-lane divided urban arterial road that connects the southeast corner of the Guiyang urban core, Youzhajie, to Xiaobi in Longdongbao; B) rural transport - the construction or rehabilitation of rural roads and rural bus stations; and, C) institutional development - develop the institutions of Guiyang municipality through studies, improvement of systems and training.
