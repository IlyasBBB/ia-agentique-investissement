Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the Project are: (a) to increase the capacity of the institutions in the Borrower's water sector; (b) to prepare the urban water supply and sanitation systems of five cities for private sector management; (c) to reorient and to reform the management and implementation of rural water supply and sanitation and to improve availability of such services to rural communities; and (d) to ensure the sustainable management of water resources.Key Indicators (as amended following MTR): Institution Building and Policy Development: - sectoral database developed - financial plan for development of DNA agreed and implementation underway - HRD strategy agreed, action plan developed and implementation underway - MIS for HRD piloted in two departments in DNA - DNA established in improved and adequate premises and staff housing constructed in Inhambane and MaputoUrban Water Supply and Sanitation: - bids invited and evaluated for private sector management of five water companies - central regulating body established and staffed in preparation for private sector management of urban water supply systems - strategic sanitation plans prepared for 7 cities and capacity of municipal staff and DNA improved for sanitation planningRural Water Supply and Sanitation: - appointment of RWTP steering committee and directorate body - RWSS Technical, Social and Operational manuals produced and field tested in Inhambane Province - approval of DAR-PRONAR institutional strengthening plan and RWTP national communication strategy - first small piped system in Inhambane rehabilitated/improved under autonomous management, with full stakeholder participation - at least 60 sustainable water points serving approximately 30,000 people constructed based on participatory design and management - piped water supplied to at least two small communities (estimated total population of 6,000) based on participatory design and appropriate management models, with tariffs designed to phase up to cover operating and maintenance cost and depreciation of at least short-life assets.Water Resources Management - Corumana Dam safety structures rehabilitated and equipment procured for regular dam maintenance - National Water Policy and Water Law reviewed and Water Resources Management Strategy formulated in consultation with stakeholders - Strategy for international waters developed

Résumé :
The National Water Development I Project seeks to increase the capacities of the organizations and people of the sector, and prepare for the private sector management of the urban water supply systems of five cities (Maputo, Beira, Quelimane, Nampula and Pemba), so that sector organizations can provide sustainable water supply and sanitation services to an increasing proportion of the community and manage water resources sustainably. There are five components in this project. First, institution building and policy development will develop a regulatory and monitoring framework and capacity, and undertake studies for development of water sector policy. Second, preparation for private sector management of urban water supply will support the bidding process for the private sector management of the water supply systems of the five cities, design and construct emergency works, prepare plans for the provision of sanitation services in the five cities. Third, rural water supply and sanitation (RWSS) will reorient and reform the management and implementation of RWSS. Fourth, water resources management will develop a national water resources management strategy. Fifth, human resources development will emphasize management training and development.
