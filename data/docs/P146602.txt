Projet : Additional Financing for Mozambique Higher Education Science and Technology Project
Identifiant : P146602
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 45.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 44 mois
Année d'approbation : 2015
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the Project are to: (a) increase the number and raise the quality of graduates at the undergraduate and graduate levels; (b) strengthen the national research capacities to produce research outputs of relevance to the Recipient’s strategic economic sectors; and (c) strengthen the institutional framework for TVET.
