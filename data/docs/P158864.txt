Projet : Support to Integrated Household Survey Program: 2018-2020
Identifiant : P158864
Pays : Malawi (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 1.69 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 22 mois
Année d'approbation : 2019
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
This project will support the Malawi Integrated Household Survey (IHS) Program over the period 2018-2020 for increasing the availability, quality, timeliness and relevance of household survey data. The support will cover partial costs of the Fifth Integrated Household Survey (IHS5) 2019/20 and the Integrated Household Panel Survey (IHPS) 2019.
