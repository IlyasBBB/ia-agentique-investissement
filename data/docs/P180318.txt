Projet : Repairing Essential Logistics Infrastructure & Network Connectivity (RELINC) Project
Identifiant : P180318
Pays : Ukraine (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 598.64 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 59 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective is to provide the means for mitigating the immediate impact that disrupted transport networks have on Ukraine’s population and economy.

Résumé :
The Repairing Essential Logistics Infrastructure and Network Connectivity (RELINC) Project is a framework operation designed to mitigate the immediate impact of disrupted transport networks on Ukraine’s population and economy. At appraisal, the Project framework envelope was estimated at US$585.0 million. The Project was approved on February 8, 2023, with an initial US$50.0 million grant from the Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction, and Reform Trust Fund (URTF) and became effective on June 21, 2023.The Project underwent its first restructuring in 2024. The restructuring: (i) allocated an additional US$230.0 million in grant financing toward the Project’s financing gap, including contributions through the URTF; (ii) introduced Component E to enhance the capacity of the Danube ports and adjusted the Project implementation arrangements accordingly; (iii) revised financing needs and allocations across components to reflect needs financed by other development partners outside RELINC and newly emerging needs for the ports; and (iv) extended the Project closing date to December 31, 2027.Following a request from the Government of Ukraine, an additional US$10.0 million was allocated from the URTF to strengthen the resilience of Ukrainian Railways (UZ) and enhance the protection of critical railway infrastructure and rolling stock. The respective Amendment to the Grant Agreement was signed and became effective in February 2026, increasing total Project financing from US$280.0 million to US$290.0 million and providing additional financing under Component B.The second restructuring responds to evolving implementation priorities and the continued need to strengthen the resilience and operational capacity of Ukraine’s transport system. It provides additional financing under Component B for the procurement of used locomotives and extends the Project closing date by 12 months, to December 31, 2028, to allow sufficient time for completion of ongoing and additional activities. The restructuring also updates the Results Framework, including relevant indicator targets and intermediate and end-target dates, to reflect the revised scope and extended implementation period.The Project comprises five components. Components A and B finance physical investments required to maintain essential transport network functionality for: (i) humanitarian relief and recovery in war-affected communities; and (ii) strengthening the resilience of economically vital westward logistics chains connecting Ukraine with EU neighbors and Moldova. Component C supports technical assistance, institutional capacity, project management, and preparation for larger and more complex recovery and reconstruction programs. Component D provides contingent emergency response capacity, if required. Component E supports improvements in the handling capacity, effectiveness, and efficiency of Danube seaports through the procurement of handling and transshipment equipment, vessels, and IT systems for traffic management at the ports of Reni and Izmail.Component A: Lifeline road connections (US$24.25 million funded; estimated framework need: US$122.25 million)A1: Modular bridges needed to restore damaged road bridge connections: Finances the procurement of modular bridge systems (approximately 500 linear meters) required for immediate and urgent restoration of damaged road bridge connections, including transport and delivery to AR staging areas and training for AR on modular bridge installation.A2: Materials required for urgent road and artificial structure repairs and winter access: Finances materials required for road and artificial structure repairs and for maintaining road access during winter, including aggregates, bitumen, salt, grit, steel, concrete, bridge components, and road-safety-related equipment, as well as delivery of such materials and equipment to AR staging areas.A3: Enhancement of western road Border Crossing Points (BCPs): Provides for equipment and technology solutions intended to facilitate operations at high-priority western road BCPs, including scanning equipment and video surveillance. Component B: Lifeline rail connections (US$323.75 million funded following the second restructuring; estimated framework need: US$405.75 million)B1: Flatbed wagons to increase containerized transport capacity: Finances the production and procurement of approximately 200 flatbed railway wagons to increase UZ’s capacity to move containerized freight.B2: Materials, components, and equipment for vital railway infrastructure: Finances materials, components, and equipment required to repair and protect vital railway infrastructure and associated systems, including rail, sleepers and fasteners, switches, power-supply equipment, and safety and signaling equipment. The additional US$10.0 million URTF financing effective in February 2026 supports materials and equipment for the protection of critical railway infrastructure and rolling stock.B3: Rolling stock for operational efficiency and resilience: Finances rolling stock required to strengthen operational efficiency, resilience, and freight capacity, including 1,435 mm gauge rolling stock, shunting locomotives, and mainline locomotives. A contract for 20 new electric locomotives is under implementation, with delivery expected in 2027. Under the proposed restructuring, additional financing will support the procurement of used locomotives to further strengthen UZ’s traction capacity and resilience.B4: Materials, equipment, and modular bridges for damaged railway bridge repairs: Provides for modular bridge systems, steel members and other materials for repair of damaged railway bridges, related training and transport, and equipment required for bridge repairs and installation of modular bridge systems.Component C: Technical support for urgently needed recovery and reconstruction initiatives and project management (US$7.5 million funded; estimated framework need: US$7.5 million)Scope: Finances: (i) costs and fees associated with UNOPS support for deployment of a portion of the Project’s initial US$50.0 million financing; (ii) technical studies, including detailed engineering designs and feasibility studies; (iii) stakeholder engagement, the sector-wide Environmental and Social Management Framework (ESMF), and other environmental and social instruments; (iv) incremental operating costs and implementation support to the relevant implementing entities; and (v) Project audits, technical supervision, remote supervision, and verification of transportation and delivery of procured goods.Component D: Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC) US$0 millionScope: Maintains a zero-dollar contingent response component that may be activated, at the Government’s request and subject to World Bank agreement, to enable rapid reallocation of Project resources in response to an eligible crisis or emergency. If activated, implementation would follow the applicable CERC Manual and related implementation, environmental and social, and fiduciary arrangements.Component E: Scaling-up Danube River Critical Logistics Chains (US$34.5 million funded; estimated framework need: US$49.5 million)Scope: Supports improvements in Danube seaport handling capacity and the effectiveness and efficiency of port operations at Reni and Izmail through the procurement of handling and transshipment equipment, vessels, and IT systems for port traffic management.E1: Handling and transshipment equipment.E2: Vessels.E3: IT systems for port management.
