Projet : India Immunization Strengthening Project - Supplement
Identifiant : P081991
Pays : India (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 83.41 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 25 mois
Année d'approbation : 2003
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed supplemental development credit to India, for US$83.0 million equivalent will supplement the ongoing Immunization Strengthening Project (CR. 3340-IN) and will assist in eradicating polio from India.As India was pursuing the goal of polio eradication by 2002, the outbreak of the epidemic in that year was not envisaged and not planned for. The country needs the additional funds to intensity and sustain polio eradication efforts. This supplementary credit will finance the procurement of oral polio vaccine (OPV) to meet the challenge. Any disruption in the supply of the vaccine at this stage would jeopardize government's efforts to eradicate polio from India which will also impede its eradication from the world.

Résumé :
The supplemental credit is consistent with the original project development objective, i.e., to eradicate poliomyelitis from India, through intensified supplemental activities, and fully meets the requirements for supplemental financing. Most importantly among the rationale for a Supplemental Credit, is that processing a free standing project runs the risk of a prolonged, and highly detrimental delay in providing immunization to the needy children. The Reproductive and Child Health Project, which will include immunization, and will be supported by the International Development Association (IDA), is expected to be processed for approval by the end of 2004, but the Government needs the additional funds by December 2003. The human, and financial costs of delay can be very high, in the form of increasing polio incidence; additionally, supplemental financing avoids the risk of disruption, and loss of momentum in India's implementation of the final phase of the polio eradication effort. Supply of additional polio vaccine under the proposed supplemental credit, will assist in the achievement of the original project objective of polio eradication.
