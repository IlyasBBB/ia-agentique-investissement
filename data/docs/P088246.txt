Projet : Cyclone Emergency Recovery Project
Identifiant : P088246
Pays : Samoa (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 6.03 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 50 mois
Année d'approbation : 2004
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives are to assist the Borrower to recover from the damage caused by Cyclone Heta and reduce the vulnerability of its people to prevailing natural hazards through:(a) Recovery of coastal resilience to erosion, flooding, and landslide hazards through appropriate and sustainable use of structural and non-structural protection;(b) Nurturing the recovery of fragile coastal ecosystems through sustainable practices; and(c) Rehabilitation of damaged land transport communications.
