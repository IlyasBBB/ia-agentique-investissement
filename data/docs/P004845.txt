Projet : VN - MEKONG DELTA WATER
Identifiant : P004845
Pays : Viet Nam (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 147.6 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 104 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project aims to promote agricultural (including aquaculture) production and reduce rural poverty through improved irrigation and drainage, increased cropping intensity and provision of clean rural water for drinking and domestic uses, and through improved living conditions brought by flood mitigation, rural water supply and improved sanitation facilities, as well as facilitate sustainable water resources development and management.

Résumé :
The Mekong Delta Water Resources Project aims at improving the water resources infrastructure in five subproject areas, divided by hydrological boundaries. The prevention of salinity intrusion on canals serving the agricultural areas, and the improvement of existing irrigation systems, for those subprojects located in the lower delta, are the main objectives. The main issues concerning the Omon-Xano subproject, located in the mid-delta, are to improve flood protection and drainage. The project will facilitate local water and land transport, and will finance land acquisition, compensation, and resettlement expenses, incurred in as a consequence of the project. The project's main components will: 1) improve water resources infrastructure, through construction of primary, secondary, and tertiary canals, together with the necessary embankments, bridges, and roads. Compensation for land acquisition and resettlement will be provided, as well as training to affected families in drainage management systems; 2) improve rural water supply and sanitation, through clean water provision and better sanitation processes; 3) strengthen water resources management and improve environmental monitoring through institutional capacity building. The component will manage engineering design, implementation and supervision, and provide institutional technical assistance and training.
