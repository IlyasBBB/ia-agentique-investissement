Projet : District and Community Health Services Revitalization Operation
Identifiant : P179913
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 100.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 58 mois
Année d'approbation : 2024
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve utilization and effectiveness of essential primary health care and nutrition services at the district level with a focus on vulnerable populations in Mozambique.

Résumé :
The Program will finance provision and expansion of primary health care services including antenatal care, immunizations, nutrition, prevention of vertical transmission of HIV, malaria, tuberculosis, and family planning at district level and community level. It is expected that utilization of these services by the most vulnerable populations will improve. It will also invest in capacity building of district health services to better plan, manage and deliver quality services in their geographical areas. The program will support reforms in budget planning and execution in the context of fiscal decentralization that the country is slowly introducing. In addition, the program will invest in health centers with internment and selected district hospitals to reinforce the referral system in underserved areas.
