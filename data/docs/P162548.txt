Projet : FISCAL CONSOLIDATION SUPPORT GRANT
Identifiant : P162548
Pays : Chad (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 80.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 12 mois
Année d'approbation : 2016
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Program Development Objectives (PDOs) are to help maintain stability on the fiscal and macroeconomic fronts including to protect critical Governmental programs and set the basis for medium term reforms. This will be achieved by: (a) broadening the tax base; (b) improving the rationalization of public spending and transparency in public resource management; and (c) improving new business registration.
