Projet : Indonesia Disaster Resilience Initiatives Project (IDRIP)
Identifiant : P170874
Pays : Indonesia (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 160.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 1 mois
Année d'approbation : 2019
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve the preparedness of the central government and selected local governments for natural hazards.

Résumé :
The development objective of Disaster Resilience Initiative Project for Indonesia is to improve the preparedness of the central government and selected local governments for natural hazards. This project has three components. 1) The first component, Disaster preparedness and emergency management capacity, contains the following sub-components: (i) Disaster risk knowledge and awareness; (ii) Multi-hazard early warning system platform development; (iii) Hazard information and early warning dissemination; and (iv) Emergency management, response, and preparedness capacities. 2) The second component, Geophysical early warning services, has the following sub-components: (i) Service delivery systems; (ii) Institutional strengthening and capacity development; and (iii) Monitoring networks and early warning capacity. 3) The third component, Project Implementation Support, aims to strengthen the capacity of implementing agencies, especially National Disaster Management Authority (BNPB) as the executing agency, to oversee project implementation at the national and subnational levels.
