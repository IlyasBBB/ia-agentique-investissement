Projet : Somali Core Economic Institutions and Opportunities Program (SCORE)
Identifiant : P152241
Pays : Somalia, Federal Republic of (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 28.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 56 mois
Année d'approbation : 2016
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objectives are to: (i) improve the enabling environment for private and financial sector development; and (ii) catalyze private investment and job creation.

Résumé :
The objectives of the Core Economic Institutions and Opportunities Program Project for Somalia are to: (a) improve the enabling environment for private and financial sector development; and (b) catalyze private investment and job creation. There are three components to the project, the first component being strengthening core economic institutions. The first component will help strengthen economic institutions in three critical areas of the economy: (a) central banking, to advance financial system supervision and regulation, which will improve financial market integrity, payment and market infrastructure resulting in safe and affordable access to a range of financial services; (b) the Ministries of Commerce and Industry, to support the introduction of a basic and more modern legal and regulatory framework that will reduce transaction costs for starting and operating businesses; and (c) port and customs authorities, to leverage private capital, networks and expertise to improve performance of the port sector. The second component is the expanding economic opportunities. Component two will provide direct support to the private sector, through technical assistance and grants, to expand economic and investment opportunities in the country. The catalytic fund will build upon mostly positive experiences of a similar fund established in Somaliland (the SBF). The SME Service Facility (SMEF) is a direct outcome of the lessons learned and public and private sector requests to complement matching grants with more extensive TA to the private sector. Finally, the third component is the project management.
