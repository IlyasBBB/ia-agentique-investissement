Projet : Productive Safety Net Project 6
Identifiant : P511478
Pays : Ethiopia (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 2375.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 70 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve access to better jobs and livelihoods, build the resilience of the poorest households, and improve the effectiveness of the adaptive social protection system

Résumé :
PSNP 6 will be transformative with a focus on building resilience through jobs and livelihoods creation enhancing the productivity of rural Ethiopians living in poverty. The project will also focus on improving human capital outcomes of beneficiaries and their households, as well as enhancing an adaptive social protection system to ensure adaptability and flexibility in responding to shocks. Ultimately, this project will contribute towards reducing rural poverty in Ethiopia.
