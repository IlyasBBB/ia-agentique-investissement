Résumé :
The development objective of Social Safety Net Enhancement Project for Iraq is to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs’ (MOLSA) programs through: (i) modernizing and strengthening the cash transfer program’s delivery systems; and (ii) establishing a MOLSA social registry that would be integrated into the national single registry. This project has two components. 1) The first component, Strengthening the building blocks of MOLSA’s programs with a focus on the CTP system and the development of MOLSA’s social registry, has the following sub-components: (i) Upgrading the key functions along the delivery chain of the CTP ; and (ii) Support the development of MOLSA social registry. 2) The second component, Project Management and Monitoring, aims to support the establishment of a project management Unit (PMU) at MOLSA to execute the project.
