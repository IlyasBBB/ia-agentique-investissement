Projet : Emergency Demobilization and Reintegration Project
Identifiant : P078288
Pays : Angola (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 258.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 2003
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The overall objective of the ADRP is to help consolidate economic stability in Angola and in the greater Great Lakes region. The two principal objectives of the program are: (i) to demobilize up to an estimated 105,000 UNITA and 33,000 FAA ex-combatants and to help support their return into civilian life; and (ii) to facilitate the reallocation of Government expenditure from military to social and economic sectors.
