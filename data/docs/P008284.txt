Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project has been restructured with the help of additional financing to finan ce a cost overrun due to higher costs for the rehabilitation and completion of t he irrigation and drainage infrastructure works under the original project. The cost overrun mainly occurred due to: (i) an increase during the last few years in the price of construction materials, in particular steel and cement, and fuel ; and (ii) a greater than anticipated volume of rehabilitation work for one comp onent of the project. The Additional Financing is required to fund all the orig inally planned activities in full, specifically the rehabilitation and enlargeme nt of the Samur-Apsheron Canal (SAC) main supply system from km 27 to 50. It wo uld thus mainly be used to carry out the last civil works contract, but there wo uld also be small allocations for the financing of consultants. The original pr oject development objectives and expected outcomes remain unchanged.

Résumé :
The Rehabilitation and Completion of Irrigation and Drainage Infrastructure Project aims to prevent the decline in supplying water to Baku City, eliminate further deterioration of the supply of irrigation water to approximately 86,000 hectares (ha) along the Sumur-Apsheron Canal; and improve drainage and reduce water logging and salinity on approximately 36,500 ha along Main-Mugan Collector. The project constitutes three components: 1) Rehabilitation and completion of irrigation and drainage infrastructure will support rehabilitation of critical main infrastructure of the Samur-Apsheron Canal (SAC) System, which comprises a headworks across the Samur river, a main supply canal, a reservoir near Baku City, pumping stations, three sediment basins, and a number of secondary canals that supply water to the irrigation areas. 2) Institutional strengthening will support institutional development to make a start with the improvement of the quality and sustainability of irrigation management by supporting technical assistance to review the work practice of SAC and training. 3) Project implementation support will cover part of the incremental staff salaries and operational expenses of the Project Implementation Unit, and the annual monitoring and evaluation.
