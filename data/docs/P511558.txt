Projet : Mozambique Health Emergency Preparedness, Response and Resilience Project Using the Multiphase Programmatic Approach
Identifiant : P511558
Pays : Mozambique (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 201.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 64 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To strengthen health system resilience and multisectoral preparedness and response to health emergencies in the Republic of Mozambique

Résumé :
The proposed Mozambique HEPRR Project (MZ HEPRR) corresponds to Phase 7 of the HEPRR MPA (P180127). The project aims to strengthen the preparedness and resilience of the health system to effectively manage health emergencies. Component 1 focuses on enhancing human resources for health (HRH) by improving governance systems, training healthcare professionals, and integrating digital health solutions. This includes the establishment of a digital HRH information management system, policies for equitable staffing, updated curricula incorporating emergency preparedness, and gender-inclusive recruitment strategies. Additionally, the component supports the development of local pharmaceutical manufacturing, improves health commodity supply chains, and establishes a transparent e-procurement system. Component 2 seeks to improve early detection and response to health emergencies through a multisectoral approach. This involves leveraging artificial intelligence for predictive analytics, enhancing integrated surveillance systems, expanding laboratory capacity, and improving emergency coordination. A key focus is ensuring the continuity of essential health services, including maternal health and gender-based violence support, especially during crises. Climate change adaptation is embedded throughout this component, with investments in climate-resilient health facilities and digital tools for early warning systems. Component 3 provides support for project management, monitoring, and evaluation. It ensures effective implementation through operational research, partner mapping, and structured assessments to track progress and impact. Meanwhile, Component 4 serves as a contingency mechanism, allowing for the rapid reallocation of project funds in response to natural disasters or crises. This ensures that emergency response measures can be swiftly enacted when needed.
