Projet : Tunisia Information and Communication Technologies Sector Development Project
Identifiant : P088929
Pays : Tunisia (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 15.37 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 78 mois
Année d'approbation : 2004
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) Sector Development Project is designed to assist Tunisia in promoting the development of its ICT sector by: (a) supporting ICT institutional and sector reforms; (b) improving its e-security mechanisms; (c) developing e-government applications; and (d) promoting the participation of the private sector in the ICT sector.

Résumé :
The Information and Communication Technologies Project will assist in the development of Tunisia's Information and Communications Technology (ICT) sector by supporting ICT institutional and sector reforms, improving its e-security mechanisms, in developing e-government applications, and, by promoting the participation of the private sector in ICT development. The four project components will: 1) assist in the development of an information and communications strategy, policy, and regulation. In particular, the component will elaborate a strategic long-term plan for developing the ICT sector, including a strategy for the promotion of universal access, and assist in the award of additional licenses, including assistance to the telecommunications regulatory authority; 2) formulate an e-government benchmarking, and strategy, being coordinated by the State Secretariat for Administrative Reform (Secretariat d'Etat a la Rbforme Administrative - SERA), and, develop e-government applications and online services in the Government's ministries and agencies, i.e., the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights (portal and on-line services), the Ministry of Culture, Youth, and Leisure (portal and on-line promotion), and the Ministry o f Communication Technologies and Transport (virtual ICT library). The component will also provide solutions to equip selected schools, and social service centers with specialized information technology (IT) solutions for the disabled, and, assist in the development of an Arabic Domain Name System (DNS) registry. ICT training shall increase capacities by providing a distance-learning platform; 3) develop and implement a computer risk-prevention strategy, based on the Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT) model, and, strengthen e-security research and training capacity. A data backup center will be established to ensure the continuity of the government's critical computer applications; and, 4) provide technical assistance (TA) for the implementation of a fast-paced project, with emphasis on results by the 2005 World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS). It will include TA to strengthen the Project Management Unit (PMU), to support an ICT statistical and economic analysis unit, will develop and implement monitoring and evaluation activities, measure progress in the sector, and, publish a regular monitoring and evaluation report. It includes procurement, IT equipment, and services, and, training.
