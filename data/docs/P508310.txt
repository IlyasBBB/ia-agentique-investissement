Projet : Armenia Housing Response and Mortgage Market Enhancement Project
Identifiant : P508310
Pays : Armenia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 250.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 60 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve refugees' access to housing solutions and enable deepening of the mortgage market.

Résumé :
The Housing Response and Mortgage Market Enhancement (HOME) Project seeks to improve refugees' access to housing solutions and enable deepening of the housing and mortgage market. The project instrument is Investment Project Financing (IPF) with Performance-Based Conditions (PBCs), organized around three components as follows: (i) Housing subsidies and support for institutional development, (ii) Housing finance market development, and (iii) Project management, communications, monitoring, and evaluation. Component 1 will provide housing solutions to eligible beneficiaries, which will simultaneously increase their resilience towards increasing climate risks such as extreme heat and floods. The component will also seek to enhance the institutional capacities of the Government of Armenia (GoA) and its relevant agencies for designing, establishing, implementing, and managing future national housing sector policy and programs. Subcomponent 1.1 will finance the provision of cash assistance (CA) to eligible beneficiaries supported by the ongoing Social Assistance Program (SAP). Subcomponent 1.2 will finance direct subsidies to eligible refugee households (HHs) under the Government Housing Support Program (HSP) for the acquisition of housing solutions through participating financial institutions (PFIs). This subcomponent will finance the provision of: (i) up-front downpayment assistance of up to 30 percent of the housing purchase price, and (ii) assistance for full monthly mortgage payments for up to the first five years of 10-year mortgage loans. Subcomponent 1.3 will finance procurement of goods, consulting, and non-consulting services for technical assistance (TA), advisory, and capacity building to support institutional strengthening and policy reforms at the national level in the housing sector as well as improvements to the HSP’s management information system managed by the Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs (MLSA). Component 2 will support the National Mortgage Company (NMC) operations by enhancing long-term liquidity for mortgage-originating financial institutions. This component aims to expand a sustainable housing finance system by providing longer-term local currency funding, essential for affordable mortgage markets. A line of credit (LOC) extended by the project to the NMC will increase access to mortgage loans by providing refinancing for PFIs, i.e., commercial banks with signed agreements with the GoA to provide mortgage loans under the HSP. Component 3 will finance the procurement of consulting and non-consulting services, goods, training, and overall operating costs to support the project implementing team (PIT) under MLSA in project management, communications, and implementation activities, including for, but not limited to, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, audits, independent verification of PBCs, procurement, financial management, environmental and social management, feedback and grievance redress mechanism (FGRM), citizen engagement activities including beneficiary surveys, and project communication outreach services. The component will also finance the operating costs for maintaining a fully staffed and equipped PIT housed within the MLSA.
