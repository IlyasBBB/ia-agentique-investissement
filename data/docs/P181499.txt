Projet : SIKASSO- KORHOGO-BOBO-DIOULASSO (SKBO) BASIN OF INTEGRATION PROJECT
Identifiant : P181499
Pays : Burkina Faso (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 200.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 68 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Improve transport connectivity and logistics services to support agricultural value chains and territorial development in the SKBo Basin

Résumé :
The development objective of Sikasso- Korhogo-Bobo-Dioulasso Basin of Integration Project for Burkina Faso is to improve climate resilient multimodal connectivity, rural accessibility, and agro-logistics in project regions. This project has four components. The first component, Resilient multimodal connectivity, has the following sub-components: Corridor Improvement for Banfora - Sidéradougou - Ouo road; and Resilient upgrade of the selected sections of the Ouagadougou - CIV border railway. The second component, Support to value-chains and climate-resilient rural accessibility, aims to finance the development of climate-resilient rural accessibility, improving transport infrastructure and building complementary facilities to enhanced climate-resilient access and logistics for rural communities and selected agricultural value chains. The third component, Project Management and Coordination, will finance the project's operating costs, monitoring and evaluation (M&S), safeguards and participatory approach activities. The fourth component, Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC), aims to allow for rapid reallocation of credit uncommitted funds in the event of an eligible emergency.
