Objectif de développement (PDO) :
First Additional Financing (P114838)The project development objectives are to support Moldova’s Economic Growth and Poverty Reduction Strategy Paper (EGPRSP) and to empower poor communities and vulnerable population groups to manage their priority development needs. The EGPRSP ran its term to expiration and has been replaced by the National Development Strategy (NDS).Second Additional Financing (P117301)To contribute to employment and wage incomes in selected poor rural communities during the current economic contraction and during the recovery.
