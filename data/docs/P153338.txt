Projet : MX: Expanding Rural Finance
Identifiant : P153338
Pays : Mexico (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 405.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2015
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project’s development objective is to expand the availability of finance to the rural economy.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Expanding Rural Finance Project for Mexico is to expand the availability of finance to the rural economy. The project comprises of two components. The first component, expanding credit for rural micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) consists of following two sub-components: (i) credit line through participating financial intermediary (PFIs) to MSMEs; and (ii) supporting rural financial development agency (FND) pilots for rural finance. The second component, strengthening institutional capacity for sustainable rural finance consists of following two sub-components: (i) modernization of banking systems; and (ii) strengthening rural financial institutions.
