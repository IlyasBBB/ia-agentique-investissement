Identifiant : P146877

Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 5.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 66 mois
Année d'approbation : 2013
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Development Objective is to improve the system of in-service teacher training and to strengthen themanagement of human resources in the education sector in São Tome and Principe.

Résumé :
The objective of the Second Phase of Quality Education for All Project for the Democratic Republic of Sao Tome and Principe is to improve the system of in-service teacher training and to strengthen the management of human resources in the education sector in Sao Tome and Principe. The project has two components: 1) improving the system of in-service teacher training in primary education; and 2) strengthening management of human resources in the education sector. The first component has two inter-related sub-components: (i) setting up in-service teacher training system; and (ii) building a student learning assessment in primary education. The second component's sub-components are: (a) strengthening management of human resources in the education sector, and (b) project management. The funds for this project are provided by the International Development Association (IDA), and this document pertains to the provision of two such grants for the project.
