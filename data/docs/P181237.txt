Projet : Strengthening Public Financial Management II Project
Identifiant : P181237
Pays : Micronesia, Federated States of (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 5.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve government capacity to manage public resources efficiently and increase transparency and accountability of service delivery.

Résumé :
The development of objective of the Second Strengthening Public Financial Management Project for Micronesia is to improve fiscal management practices and increase fiscal transparency at the national government level. The project comprises of four components. The first component improved fiscal management practices and controls will support the improvement of practices to carry out budgeting, financial reporting, internal audit and procurement within Department of Finance and Administration (DOFA). It consists of following sub-components: (i) strengthening budgeting and reporting practices; and (ii) strengthening procurement practices. The second component expanded core financial management information systems will support increased efficiency and automation of public finance processes through the expanded use of the financial management information system (FMIS) implemented under public financial management (PFM) I to additional government Departments and agencies. It consists of following sub-components: (i) continued rollout of core public finance system; and (ii) expand public finance systems. The third component, enhanced human resource capacity, communications, and change management aims to build on the initiatives to strengthen human resource capacity, foster communications and carry out change management established under PFM I by continuing competency development activities, engaging in continued internal and external communications, and carrying out in citizen engagement activities. It consists of following sub-components: (i) strengthen human resource capacity for public finance; and (ii) change management, communications and citizen engagement. The fourth component, project management objective is to support overall project management and coordination.
