Projet : BR: AIDS & STD Control II
Identifiant : P054120
Pays : Brazil (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 300.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 57 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
(1) AIDS AND STD PREVENTION ACTIVITIES; (2) IMPROVEMENTS IN THE DIAGNOSIS, TREATMENT AND CARE FOR HIV, AIDS AND STD PATIENTS; AND (3) STRENGTHENING OF PUBLIC AND PRIVATE INSTITUTIONS FOR AIDS/STD CONTROL.

Résumé :
The objectives of the Second Aids and STD Control Project are: 1) to reduce the incidence of HIV and STD infection and 2) expand and improve diagnosis, treatment, and care of persons with HIV, AIDS, and STD. The project components include: 1) AIDS and STD prevention, 2) diagnosis, treatment and care for HIV, AIDS, and STD patients, and 3) strengthening of public and private institutions for AIDS/STD control.
