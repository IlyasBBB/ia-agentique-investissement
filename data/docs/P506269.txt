Projet : Safeguarding Access to Fiscal and Educational Resources Project
Identifiant : P506269
Pays : Sao Tome and Principe (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 24.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 18 mois
Année d'approbation : 2024
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective (PDO) are to support continued access to education and contribute to addressing sources of fiscal pressure emanating from the energy sector in Sao Tome and Principe.

Résumé :
The proposed project will enable the continued delivery of services in the education sector by ensuring the payment of salaries to teachers and school administrators, and support critical activities in the energy sector that will further its sustainability and limit its negative fiscal impacts. By supporting the education sector, the proposed project directly addresses the highest expenditure for the state.
