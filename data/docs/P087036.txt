Durée prévue : 51 mois
Année d'approbation : 2005

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective is to support the Ministry of Finance's effort s to improve fiscal sustainability and quality of public expenditures, by focusi ng on the Ministry?s strategic definitions and objectives and strengthening core products and increasing efficiency, effectiveness and transparency of its inter nal administrative and financial management systems, to become a Ministry of Exc ellence and serve as a model for overall government reform.

Résumé :
The Modernization of the Ministry of Finance Project for Paraguay aims at improving fiscal sustainability and quality of public expenditures through the strengthening of its core functions and responsibilities, and the improvement of internal administrative and financial management systems, so as to enable the Ministry of Finance (MoF) to assist the Government in the reform of its public sector. The project has the following four components: Component 1) will support the improvement of fiscal and economic planning for policy formulation, implementation and monitoring. Component 2) will provide technical assistance for the diagnosis, design and implementation of organization reforms, training and monitoring for: change management, process streamlining, information management, human resources management, and internal control and auditing. Component 3) will support: transparency, civil society participation, and communications. Component 4) The Project Coordination Unit (PCU) will provide support to the Ministry of Finance in designing, implementing and monitoring policies and institutional reforms.
