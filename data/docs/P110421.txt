Projet : Rural Investment & Services Project 2 - Additional Financing
Identifiant : P110421
Pays : Moldova (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 6.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 54 mois
Année d'approbation : 2008
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project is the second Phase of a two-phase Rural Investment and Services Program. The Program objective is to provide long-term support to accelerate agricultural recovery and growth so that Moldova's agricultural and rural sectors can play their full role in providing the underpinnings for future income growth and poverty reduction. Under this overarching objective, the project will strive to continue foster the post-privatization growth in the agricultural and rural sectors of Moldova by improving access of farmers and rural entrepreneurs to legal ownership status, know-how, knowledge and financial services, while building the capacity of the private and public institutions to ensure the sustainability of the activities.
