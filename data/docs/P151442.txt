Projet : Social Assistance Project
Identifiant : P151442
Pays : Lesotho (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 27.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2016
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to support the GoL in improving the efficiency, equity and shock responsive function of selected Social Assistance programs and, in the event of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, to provide immediate and effective response to said Eligible Crisis or Emergency.

Résumé :
The development objective of Social Assistance Project for Lesotho is to support the Government of Lesotho in improving the efficiency and equity of selected social assistance programs. This project has two components. 1) The first component, Strengthening administrative efficiency and equity of social assistance programs, aims to support the results in efficiency and equity of selected social assistance programs, in line with the National Social Protection Strategy (NSPS) and its implementation framework. 2) The second component, Implementation Support, aims to support the investment necessary to overcome the main constraints in term of capacity, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of results supported by first component and to support the design and implementation of a pilot of livelihood enhancement activities among beneficiaries of social assistance programs to support further household’s income generating capacity in the medium and long term. It has three subcomponents as follows: (i) Supporting the achievement of results in selected social assistance programs; (b) Supporting the design and implementation of a pilot of livelihoods enhancement activities; and (iii) Supporting the project management.
