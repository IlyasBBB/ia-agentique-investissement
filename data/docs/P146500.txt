Projet : Emergency 2007 Cyclone Recovery and Restoration Project-- AF II
Identifiant : P146500
Pays : Bangladesh (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 140.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 54 mois
Année d'approbation : 2013
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The PDO is to support the Government of Bangladesh efforts to facilitate recovery from the damage to livelihoods and infrastructure caused by Cyclone Sidr and to build long-term preparedness through strengthened disaster risk reduction and management.
