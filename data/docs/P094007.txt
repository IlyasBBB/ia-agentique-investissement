Projet : Morocco - Second Rural Roads Project
Identifiant : P094007
Pays : Morocco (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 654.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 134 mois
Année d'approbation : 2006
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to increase rural populations' access to all-weather roads in support of the Guarantor's Program.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Second Rural Roads Project is to increase rural populations' access to all-weather roads by supporting the first Phase of the Government's Second National Program of Rural Roads (NPRR-2). The project will contribute, namely, to increase the national rural road accessibility indicator - NRRAI (defined as the percentage of population living within one kilometer from an all-weather road) from 54 percent in 2005, to 67 percent in 2010. The project consists of two components involving the rehabilitation/upgrading of about 7,800 km o f rural roads during the period 2005-2010. The first component - Rural roads financed by the CFR (Caisse de FinancementRoutier) - will support the rehabilitation or upgrading of some 4,600 km of rural roads (including small, complementary road-related infrastructure), with an estimated cost of about US$385 million. The second component, - Rural roads financed by the DRCR (General Budget and Road Fund) - will support the rehabilitation or upgrading of some 3,200 km of rural roads (including small, complementary road-related infrastructure), through DRCR budget and the Road Fund, with an estimated cost of about US$269 million.
