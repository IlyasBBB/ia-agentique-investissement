Projet : ROAD MAINTENANCE SUPPLEMENTAL CREDIT
Identifiant : P083897
Pays : Albania (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 16.22 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 48 mois
Année d'approbation : 2003
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The proposed supplemental credit would be used to return the investment program for the Road Maintenance Project to its original size.

Résumé :
This Supplemental Credit will be used to return the investment program for the Road Maintenance Project for Albania to its original size, improving about 1,000 km of the national road network and about 1,000 km o f the rural road network. No changes to the originally negotiated Credit are proposed. The Project is expected to be completed by the original Closing Date.
