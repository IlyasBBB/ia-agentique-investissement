Projet : NI ENHANCED COMPETITIVENESS FOR INTERNATIONAL MARKET INTEGRATION
Identifiant : P092949
Pays : Nicaragua (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 17.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 12 mois
Année d'approbation : 2006
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective is to strengthen the capacity of the CPC to provide technical leadership and coordinate the efforts of government agencies, municipalities, the private sector, civil society and donors in the field of competitiveness.

Résumé :
The Enhanced Competitiveness for International Market Integration Project for Nicaragua aims to strengthen the capacity of the Presidential Commission of Competitiveness (CPC) to provide technical leadership and coordinate the efforts of government agencies, municipalities, the private sector, civil society and donors in the field of competitiveness. The project consists of four components: (i) business climate improvement; (ii) business services; (iii) strengthening financial services; and (iv) capacity building of the CPC. The first component supports the government's role in supporting private sector competitiveness. The second component offers services to firms (predominantly SMEs). The third component will strengthen Superintendency of Banks and other Financial Institutions (SIBOIF's) ability to supervise the financial sector and will upgrade the payments system. Finally, the fourth component will improve the ability of the CPC to manage and coordinate competitiveness plans and activities.
