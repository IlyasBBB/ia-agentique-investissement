Projet : Second Emergency Demobilization and Reintegration Project
Identifiant : P112712
Pays : Rwanda (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 19.09 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 100 mois
Année d'approbation : 2009
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to support the efforts o f the Recipient to: (i) demobilize members of armed groups o f Rwandan origin and members of the Rwandan Defense Force; and (ii)p rovide socioeconomic reintegration support to said members following demobilization, with a particular focus on the provision of such support to female, child, and disabled ex-combatants.
