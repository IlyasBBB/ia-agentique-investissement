Projet : Privatization Support Project
Identifiant : P049735
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 16.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 49 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT WILL HELP (A) STRENGTHEN THE REPUBLIC OF YEMEN'S INSTITUTIONAL CAPACITY FOR PRIVATIZATION; (B) PREPARE ASMALL NUMBER OF LARGE TRANSACTIONS FOR PRIVATIZATION; AND (C) PROVIDE PROGRAMMATIC SUPPORT FORTHE PRIVATIZATION OF SMALLER ASSETS.

Résumé :
The Privatization Support Project, will back privatization transactions, raise private enterprise efficiency, and reduce the fiscal, and monetary burden of the public enterprise sector. The components are: 1) The institutional support for program, and project management, will provide foreign consulting, technical assistance, and training to strengthen the Technical Privatization Office, and concerned ministries, in privatization management. Construction works will upgrade facilities, and equipment supply will be procured. 2) The transactions support for large enterprises, include financial/legal advisory services for the sale of large scale enterprise assets. Thus, it is expected that international consultants will address associated policy, and regulatory issues towards competitive market practices, on the sale of at least three of the five largest government enterprises. 3) The transactions support for small, and medium enterprises, will provide financial, and legal advisory services, for sale preparations of some fifty small/medium enterprise assets, as well as for marketing, and procurement of those assets, including the necessary associated policy, and regulatory practice services. 4) The pre-privatization strategies for large enterprises, encompass the process, and methodology for the privatization of the five large enterprises, initiating comprehensive policy, and institutional frameworks.
