Projet : Supplementary Development Policy Financing: Sustaining Market Reforms in Uzbekistan
Identifiant : P173948
Pays : Uzbekistan (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 200.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 8 mois
Année d'approbation : 2020
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Sustain Uzbekistan's economic reforms and transition to a market economy by (i) increasing the role of markets and the private sector in the economy; and (ii) enhancing social inclusion.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Supplementary Development Policy Financing for Uzbekistan is to sustain Uzbekistan’s economic reforms and transition to a market economy by: (i) increasing the role of markets and the private sector in the economy; and (ii) enhancing social inclusion. The supplemental financing loan is an early component of a coordinated donor strategy to fill the budget financing gap in 2020 and meet immediate Coronavirus 2019 (COVID-19) - related spending needs. At the government’s request, these operations are being prepared in close consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), which stands ready to provide additional support if worsening conditions create a balance of payments need. Further resources from all three institutions are likely to be available should conditions deteriorate even further.
