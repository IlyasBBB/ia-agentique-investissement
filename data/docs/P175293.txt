Projet : Senegal: DPF2 - Equitable and Resilient Recovery in Senegal
Identifiant : P175293
Pays : Senegal (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 300.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 7 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The development objective of this programmatic DPO is to enable equitable and sustainable growth by: (I) increasing resilience and productivity of households, and (ii) strengthening transparent and sustainable use of productive resources, and efficient management of fiscal accounts.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Second Equitable and Resilient Recovery Development Policy Financing for Senegal is to enable equitable and sustainable growth by: (i) increasing resilience and productivity of households, and (ii) strengthening transparent and sustainable use of productive resources, and efficient management of fiscal accounts. The program consists of policy reforms that will sustainably and equitably broaden access to healthcare, social assistance, and economic opportunities for women (pillar 1); and improve debt management and equitable tax revenue mobilization, while boosting the growth potential of the private sector, with a focus on access to finance and competitive private sector participation in the digital and energy sectors (pillar 2).
