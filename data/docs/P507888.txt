Projet : Community and Local Development in Western Côte d'Ivoire Project
Identifiant : P507888
Pays : Cote d'Ivoire (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 100.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 52 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve access to inclusive and resilient basic services and economic opportunities in Western Cote d’Ivoire.

Résumé :
The proposed operation seeks to improve access to inclusive and resilient basic services and economic opportunities in Western Cote d’Ivoire.The project is structured around the following core components:Component 1: Resilient and inclusive local development at the community level finances (i) climate resilient, small-scale community infrastructure for health, and other basic services selected by communities and relying on community labor ; and (ii) targeted support to women and youth economic groups for income-generating activities through direct financing and technical assistance from implementing partners.Component 2: Socio-economic development at the regional level finances (i) regional-level economic infrastructures (markets, granaries etc.), based on value chains assessments to strengthen economic development, connectivity; and (ii) local investments to rehabilitate and improve existing water points (pump repair/replacement, technician refresher training, capacitating water committees).Component 3: Knowledge generation and institutional strengthening finances (i) community behavioral interventions focused on social cohesion to ensure equitable, transparent access to shared services , support local mediation at village and sous prefecture levels ; (ii) analytical work to identify potential institutionalization pathways for CLD in Cote d’Ivoire and (iii) Capacity building for local stakeholders.Component 4: Project management finances staffing and operational costs to ensure effective, transparent delivery through robust coordination, safeguards, fiduciary management, M&E, and beneficiary feedback mechanisms.
