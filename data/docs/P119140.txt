Projet : 4th Northwest Mountainous & Forested Areas Development Project (PNO4)
Identifiant : P119140
Pays : Tunisia (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Agriculture

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objectives (PDOs) are to improve the socio-economic conditions of the rural population and promote better protection and management of natural resources in the project area using an integrated participatory approach to community-based development.
