Projet : Improvement of Solid Waste Management to Support Regional and Metropolitan Cities
Identifiant : P157245
Pays : Indonesia (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 100.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 90 mois
Année d'approbation : 2019
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve solid waste management services for urban populations in selected cities across Indonesia.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Improvement of Solid Waste Management to Support Regional and Metropolitan Cities Project for Indonesia is to improve solid waste management services for urban populations in selected cities across Indonesia. The project comprises of four components. The first component, institutional and policy development will support institutional strengthening and capacity building of central government agencies responsible for various technical and administrative aspects of solid waste management services. The second component, integrated planning support and capacity building for local government and communities will finance the costs of experts and community facilitators throughout the program cycle to support capacity building (including longer-term management support, training, workshops, and knowledge exchange events between cities as well as urban sub-districts) of local governments and communities to design and manage solid waste service improvements. The third component, solid waste infrastructure in selected cities will also be supported with the required technical assistance package through component 2. It consists of following sub-components: (i) support for integrated solid waste management systems for Citarum watershed cities; and (ii) supporting integrated solid waste management systems in selected cities, other than Citarum watershed cities. The fourth component, implementation support and technical assistance will finance the program management during the implementation, construction supervision consultants, monitoring and evaluation, and specific technical assistance for cities and district governments receiving the investment of component 3.
