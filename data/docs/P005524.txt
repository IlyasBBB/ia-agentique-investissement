Projet : FES MEDINA REHABILITATION PROJECT
Identifiant : P005524
Pays : Morocco (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 17.93 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 85 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
'The objectives of the project are to assist in the conservation and rehabilitation of the Medina of Fes, with particular attention to its historic built heritage and urban environment, through the expansion and acceleration of ongoing conservation efforts, the consolidation of partnerships between the private and public sectors, and the alleviation of poverty through community development and the promotion of new growth oriented activities'(Schedule 2, Description of the Project, Loan agreement, Loan 4403-MOR)At MTR the Bank and the Borrowers agreed that in view of different arrangements made or to be made for the implementation of the urban environment activities by the Municipality, the related loan proceeds would be reallocated, with the exception of those activities already carried out (elimination of solid waste stocks). Private sector contributions were expected at the time of project design for the construction of parking garages, that were not considered any more feasible within the scope of the project by MTR. The formal restructuring of the project that occurred in December 2003 at the time of project extension reflected these changes, and the Loan Agreements were consequently amended.Therefore, since the MTR, the project is primarily focusing on:a) housing rehabilitation b) the implementation of the emergency network within the Medina; c) the implementation of the access roads to the Medina; and d) the completion of civil works related to public spaces and gardens, and tourism circuits

Résumé :
The Fes-Medina Rehabilitation Project for Morocco aims to assist in the conservation and rehabilitation of Fes-Medina, especially the historic housing stock and urban environment. The first establishes a rehabilitation or grant facility to support private initiatives in historic building conservation, including innovative rehabilitation of historic housing; and develops new tourism itineraries, coupled with the conservation or rehabilitation of historic buildings. The second component 1) provides access for emergency public services extending to the center of the medina; 2) improves critical links with medina entrances; 3)facilitates ease of movement for residents, businesses, tourists, and goods; 4) increases parking spaces to the level of demand; and 5) improves traffic management. The third component aims to improve solid waste collection by providing equipment; and minimize pollution by providing improved workshop facilities for the most polluting traditional activities within the medina. The fourth component rehabilitates the medina wall and launches emergency action for occupied housing falling in ruins in order to promote labor-intensive job opportunities and to promote small-scale private sector contractors. The final component finances a resource mobilization program; strengthens the agency responsible for rehabilitating Fes-Medina (ADER-Fes); sets up a conservation laboratory; and sets up a training program.
