Projet : VN-RURAL ENERGY
Identifiant : P056452
Pays : Viet Nam (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 204.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 79 mois
Année d'approbation : 2000
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
(i) expand rural access to electricity in 902 communes located in 34 provinces of Vietnam through grid extension, where economically justified, to improve welfare, enhance income earning capacity and help alleviate poverty;(ii) define and establish institutional mechanisms and strategy for rural electrification in Vietnam that would facilitate: (a) a sustained program of continued rural electrification with increased local management of operation and management, conversion of commune electricity groups to joint stock cooperatives or companies; (b) the long-term viability of the power sector through appropriate bulk supply tariffs and distribution margins; and (c) continued implementation of power sector reforms in accordance with the 1997 Policy Letter and Strategy; and(iii) promote the application of renewable energy sources in areas inaccessible to the national grid and to supplement grid power supply.

Résumé :
The Rural Energy Project for Vietnam aims to expand rural access to electricity in communes located in 32 provinces through grid extension, where economically justified, to improve welfare, enhance income-earning capacity and help alleviate poverty; 2) to define and establish institutional mechanisms and strategy for rural electrification; and 3) promote the application of renewable energy sources in areas inaccessible to the national grid and to supplement grid power supply. The first three components include the construction of transmission lines , substations, and consumer meters, etc. in northern, southern, and central regions. The fourth component provides technical assistance and capacity building for project management and coordination including staff development and training; improves management systems in the state power agency; prepares a pilot cooperative for investment, management, and operation of rural power distribution at the commune level; and lastly helps fund feasibility studies for a system and energy efficiency improvement program.
