Projet : China GEF City Cluster Eco-Transport Project
Identifiant : P121263
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 25.05 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 51 mois
Année d'approbation : 2012
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To support the Government of China in enhancing transport efficiency, saving energy, and reducing CO2 emissions through multi-modaltransport integration in city clusters.

Résumé :
The development objective of the Global Environment Facility (GEF) City Cluster Eco Transport Project for China is to assist the Government of China in enhancing transport efficiency, energy saving and CO2 emission reduction, by promoting and demonstrating multi modal transport integration in city clusters. There are four components to the project. The first component is China city cluster eco-transport development strategic planning. This component will support consultant services to help Ministry of Transport (MOT) analyze the emerging transport patterns and issues of city clusters, multi-modal integrated transport strategies, policies, regulations, institutional framework and coordination mechanism, and develop technical guidelines for city cluster integrated transport planning and multi-modal passenger terminal design. The second component is pilot demonstration in Changsha-Zhuzhou-Xiangtan (CZT) city cluster. This component will support the activities, which will be carried out in parallel and in coordination with the implementation of first component. The third component is capacity building. This component supports the training and capacity building activities. The fourth component is project management. This component support to Project Management Office (PMO) to implement, supervise and manage the different parts of the project.
