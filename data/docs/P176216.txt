Projet : Additional Financing for the Tajikistan Emergency COVID-19 Project
Identifiant : P176216
Pays : Tajikistan (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 12.57 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 40 mois
Année d'approbation : 2021
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
Project Development Objective (PDO) is to prevent, prepare and respond to the COVID-19 pandemic in the Republic of Tajikistan.

Résumé :
The proposed Additional Financing will increase the development effectiveness of the Parent Project that supports the response of the Government of Tajikistan to COVID-19 in Tajikistan by addressing gaps in both the scale and scope of the Parent Project. It will do so by strengthening the oxygen supply, providing funding for COVID-19 therapeutics and filling the gap in routine vaccines, supporting vaccine program rollout and strengthening communication activities, and expanding the Parent Project’s emergency cash assistance program.
