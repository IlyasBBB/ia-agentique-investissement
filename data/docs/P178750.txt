Projet : SENEGAL HIGHER EDUCATION PROJECT - ESPOIR-JEUNES
Identifiant : P178750
Pays : Senegal (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 150.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 61 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To increase equitable access to market-relevant short-term vocational tertiary education and strengthen governance and research in higher education.

Résumé :
The proposed project will support the ongoing and planned higher education reforms to enhance the capacity of higher education institutions (HEIs) in Senegal to produce high-quality graduates with skills aligned with labor market needs. The Project will do so by improving the governance framework for higher education while concurrently supporting the Government’s strategic policy options, including (i) expanding access to short-term tertiary vocational training programs, (ii) improving the quality and relevance of programs by strengthening quality assurance systems in place, performance requirements, and making courses more relevant to the labor market needs, (iii) strengthening internal and external efficiency; (iv) promoting greater use of information technology in tertiary education; (v) modernizing governance systems and management of the higher education system and (vi) developing research.
