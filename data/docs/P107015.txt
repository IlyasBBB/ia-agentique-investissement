Projet : Civil Service Modernization Project Supplemental Loan
Identifiant : P107015
Pays : Yemen, Republic of (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 17.32 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 27 mois
Année d'approbation : 2008
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective is to create sustained utilization of human and financial resources in the administration. To this end, the project would help put in place core personnel and financial management systems, establish a mechanism to reduce the number of unqualified civil servants, and initiate a restructuring process in individual ministries. To make this foundation as solid as possible, another objective of the project is to upgrade the management capacity in the civil service.
