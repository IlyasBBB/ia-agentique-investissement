Projet : SECOND RURAL MUNICIPAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT
Identifiant : P055823
Pays : Nicaragua (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 40.7 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 81 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective (PDO) is to build the capacity of rural municipalities in three major areas in which they can make a critical contribution to sustainable rural development: (i) provision of local public infrastructure and the delivery of municipal services; (ii) the protection of the environment and the sustainable management of natural resources, and (iii) the promotion of local economic development.

Résumé :
The project objective of the Second Rural Municipal Development Project for Nicaragua is to improve the performance of rural municipalities by building their capacity in three major areas in which municipal governments can make a crucial contribution to sustaining rural development: providing local public infrastructure and the delivery of municipal services; protecting the environment and the sustainable management of natural resources; and promoting local economic development. There are two project components: municipal development and municipal environmental management. The first component includes four sub-components, which are decentralized financing, strengthening municipal institutions, strengthening the Nicaraguan Institute for Municipal Development (INIFOM), and evaluating and developing policy. The second component includes three sub-component, which are decentralization of environmental management, environmental information systems, and environmental municipal facility.
