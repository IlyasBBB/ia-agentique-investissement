Projet : Integrated Flood Resilience and Adaptation Project
Identifiant : P180323
Pays : Pakistan (Moyen-Orient, Afrique du Nord, Afghanistan et Pakistan)
Secteur : Industrie et commerce
Coût total : 400.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 67 mois
Année d'approbation : 2023
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The project development objective (PDO) is to improve livelihoods and essential services and enhance flood risk protection in selected communities affected by the 2022 floods.

Résumé :
This additional financing of USD 155 million includes third restructuring of the Integrated Flood Resilience and Adaptation Project (IFRAP) to scale up climate-resilient housing reconstruction in Balochistan following the 2022 floods. Building on earlier restructurings that established the Housing Reconstruction Unit and partially addressed financing gaps, the proposed Level 2 restructuring will reallocate resources from Component 2 to Component 3 and introduce a new sub-component on Internship and Skills Development, while updating the Results Framework; the PDO and closing date remain unchanged. The additional financing is driven by strong implementation performance and field verification of a substantially larger pool of eligible beneficiaries than initially anticipated, with the housing component demonstrating high disbursement and utilization rates, confirming readiness for scale-up under the Series of Projects approach. The combined financing—new IDA resources and reallocation—will close the remaining gap and support climate-resilient housing grants for an additional 116,946 households, increasing total coverage to 213,946 beneficiaries and raising total project financing to US$400 million, thereby enabling full coverage of all confirmed eligible households.
