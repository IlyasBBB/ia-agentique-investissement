Projet : MG-Urban Infrastructure Project
Identifiant : P048697
Pays : Madagascar (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 45.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 96 mois
Année d'approbation : 1997
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objective of the Project is to assist in providing increased income and employment for the poor, and improving the provision and maintenance of urban infrastructure in the city of Antananarivo and other selected cities of Madagascar.

Résumé :
The development objectives of the Urban Infrastructure Project are as follows: (i) improved provision and maintenance of priority urban infrastructure; and (ii) increased income and employment for the poor. The project will comprise the following components: 1) civil works, including contracts for construction/rehabilitation of economic infrastructure and revenue earning projects; 2) equipment, consisting of laboratory and machines to produce precast cement blocks for pavement in cities where capacity is weak, as well as office equipment; 3) consultancy services to assist municipalities and beneficiaries to formulate and evaluate Priority Investment Programs of Municipalities (PIPMs) and to Agence d'Execution des Travaux d'Infrastructure Publique (AGETIP) to conduct analysis and training for small and medium enterprises (SMEs), to facilitate participatory seminars, to provide information services and to finance technical and financial audits; and 4) project management-AGETIP(s) management costs.
