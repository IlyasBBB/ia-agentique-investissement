Projet : Municipal Development Project
Identifiant : P035805
Pays : Armenia (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Eau et assainissement
Coût total : 35.49 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 92 mois
Année d'approbation : 1998
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The main objectives of the project are to: (i) make emergency improvements in the water supply system of Yerevan; (ii) improve the efficiency, management, operation and delivery of water and wastewater services for the Yerevan service area; and (iii) lay the groundwork for the sustainable involvement of the private sector in the overall management of these services in Armenia.The objectives of the housing component are to assist the Government of Armenia (GOA) to (i) develop a national housing policy focusing on critical economic issues associated with the ongoing housing sector reform; and (ii) establish a housing strategy in the Earthquake Damaged Zone (EQZ).

Résumé :
The main objectives of the Municipal Development Project are to: (a) make emergency short term improvements in the water supply system to improve the drinking water supply to Yerevan, in particular to the poorer and most affected elements of the population; (b) improve the efficiency, management, operation and delivery of water and wastewater services for the Yerevan service area; and (c) lay the groundwork for the sustainable involvement of the private sector in the overall management of these services in Armenia. The project components include: immediate investment program; management contract; operating investment fund; capital investments; housing; and technical assistance.
