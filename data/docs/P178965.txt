Projet : Third AF to The Gambia COVID-19 Vaccine Preparedness and Response Project
Identifiant : P178965
Pays : Gambia, The (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Santé
Coût total : 12.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 13 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To prevent, detect and respond to the threat posed by COVID-19 and strengthen national system for public health preparedness

Résumé :
The proposed project will support the implementation of The Gambia COVID-19 Plan endorsed by the Minister of Health (on March 6, 2020): Emergency COVID-19 Response; Strengthening Multi-sector, National Institutions and Platforms for Policy Development and Coordination of Prevention and Preparedness using One Health approach; Supporting National and Sub-national, Prevention and Preparedness; and Implementation Management and Monitoring and Evaluation
