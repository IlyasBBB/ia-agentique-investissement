Projet : Third Social Fund Project
Identifiant : P064305
Pays : Madagascar (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Protection sociale
Coût total : 17.7 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 51 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
TO FINANCE DEMAND DRIVEN COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ACTIVITIES IN RURALAREAS AND EMPOWER BENEFICIARIES.

Résumé :
The project development objectives, similar to those of the ongoing Second Social Fund project (see report number 14489), include improved access among the poor to social and economic infrastructure; empowerment on a pilot basis of poor rural communities and/or communes to identify, organize, manage funds, and implement sub-projects responsive to community needs in order to achieve decentralized social and economic development; employment creation; and improved capacity in the private sector. There are three project components: 1) communal infrastructure rehabilitation and construction; 2) capacity building for communes, rural communities/local associations, and private sector (small contractors, consultants, artisans, skilled labor and nongovernmental organizations) --in very specific cases study tours could be envisaged abroad; and 3) equipment purchases, operating costs, staff training, audits, and studies for the social development fund.
