Projet : Sustainable Cities and Land Project
Identifiant : P180884
Pays : Cameroon (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 200.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 73 mois
Année d'approbation : 2025
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve access to climate-resilient infrastructure and enhance land administration services in targeted urban areas in Cameroon.

Résumé :
The project supports unlocking potentials of urban development, livability, and land administration services in the largest cities as an essential driver of socio-economic growth in Cameroon, through a sustainable, climate-resilient, and inclusive approach. The project design is complementary to other relevant projects in Cameroon financed by the Bank and development partners. The project is structured around four integrated components:Component 1. Transformative, Climate-Resilient Urban Infrastructure Sub-component 1.1. Improvement of Urban Infrastructure Sub-component 1.2. Policy and Technical Support and Capacity Building for Sustainable Urbanization Component 2. Improvement of Urban Land Administration Systems Sub-component 2.1. Policy Dialogue and Legal Reform Sub-component 2.2. Modernization of Land Administration Services Sub-component 2.3. Piloting Land Tenure SecurityComponent 3. Project ManagementComponent 4. Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC)
