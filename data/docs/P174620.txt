Identifiant : P174620
Pays : Central African Republic (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Gouvernance

Durée prévue : 62 mois
Année d'approbation : 2022
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Project Development objective (PDO) is: To (i) improve efficiency and transparency in key areas of public resources management, and (ii) lay foundations for a trusted and inclusive digital government.

Résumé :
The development objective of Public Sector Digital Governance Project for Central African Republic is: To (i) improve efficiency and transparency in key areas of public resources management, and (ii) lay foundations for a trusted and inclusive digital government. This project has four components. The first component, Improve the management of public resources, has the following sub-components: Revenue management; Strengthening budget process and core expenditure management; Strengthening human resource and payroll management; Improving efficiency and transparency of public procurement and public investment management; and Improving development and service delivery planning. The second component, Improving development and service delivery planning, has the following sub-components: Laying the Foundations for Improved Connectivity; Strengthening Digital Government Foundations; and Strengthening Digital Government Foundations. The third component, Implementation Support, Citizen Engagement, and M&E, will support the establishment of a Project Implementation Unit (PIU) to provide overall project coordination and implementation support. The fourth component, Contingent Emergency Response, has an initial zero value but may be financed during the project to allow for agile response to emerging emergency events.
