Projet : Second Poverty Alleviation Microfinance Project (Microfinance II)
Identifiant : P059143
Pays : Bangladesh (Asie du Sud)
Secteur : Finance
Coût total : 181.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 101 mois
Année d'approbation : 2001
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
An Additional Financing to the original project was approved by the Board on May 30, 2007. The objective of the Additional Financing is to diversify microcredit operations to reach a new underserved segment of the urban poor and providing them with technical skills so that they can utilize the microcredit for productive gain to improve their livelihood. The additional funds would also continue to build institutional capacity in PKSF, the selected POs and the stakeholders.

Résumé :
The Second Poverty Alleviation Microfinance Project (Microfinance II) to Bangladesh aims to continue the International Development Association's (IDA's) support for poverty alleviation through microcredit. The Project has five main components. 1) The mainstream microfinance - rural microcredit component will continue to support horizontal and vertical expansion of rural microcredit, to respond to the unmet demand and the need for larger loans per borrower, respectively. 2) The urban microcredit component will support scaling-up of urban microcredit activities and will cover up to 270,000 urban borrowers. 3) The hard-core poor component will target program expansion, change the current microcredit delivery system, and diversify financial services. 4) The microenterprise lending component will help progressive or graduate microcredit borrowers or microentrepreneurs who have entrepreneurial talents to scale-up their activities. 5) The institutional development component will strengthen Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation's (PKSF) and small and medium-size partner organizations' (POs) capacity to increase outreach, broaden the range of financial services benefiting the poor and hard-core poor, and improve POs' creditworthiness and sustainability.
