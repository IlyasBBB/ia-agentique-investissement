Projet : Biodiversity Conservation Management Project
Identifiant : P044176
Pays : Romania (Europe et Asie centrale)
Secteur : Agriculture
Coût total : 8.8 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 88 mois
Année d'approbation : 1999
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The Globa l objective of the project is sustainable conservation of the biological diversity and ecological integrity of the Romanian forest, alpine and meadow ecosystems of the Carpathian mountain chain.The development objective of the project is to establish effective inter-sectoral, participatory planning and sustainable management of natural ecosystems and associated landscapes at three demonstration sites in the Carpathian mountains and mechanisms to support replication of these activities at other priority sites.
