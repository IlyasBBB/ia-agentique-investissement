Projet : H & T EDUCATION
Identifiant : P001923
Pays : Mauritius (Afrique de l'Est et australe)
Secteur : Éducation
Coût total : 26.5 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 43 mois
Année d'approbation : 1995
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
THE PROJECT WILL ASSIST THE GOVERNMENT TO IMPLEMENT TERTIARY EDUCATION MASTER PLAN, PARTICULARLY (1) UNIVERSITY SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING AND (2) POLYTECHNIC EDUCATION TO IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF THE LABOR FORCE TO ENABLE THE COUNTRY TO SUSTAIN A HIGH TECHNOLOGY AND SKILLS-INTENSIVE ECONOMY.

Résumé :
The Higher and Technical Education Project will support the Government's education sector program for higher and polytechnic education, which aims to produce the human resources required to support a more competitive economy. The project will assist the Government's program by: (a) strengthening the University of Mauritius, and (b) rationalizing polytechnic education.
