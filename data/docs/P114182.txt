Projet : Provincial Energy Efficiency Scale-up Program
Identifiant : P114182
Pays : China (Asie de l'Est et Pacifique)
Secteur : Énergie
Coût total : 300.74 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 69 mois
Année d'approbation : 2011
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To improve the quality and sustainability of provincial energy efficiency programs in Shandong, Shanxi and Jiangxi Provinces through technical assistance and institutional capacity building

Résumé :
The development objective of the Provincial Energy Efficiency Scale-up Project is to improve quality and sustainability of provincial energy efficiency programs in Shandong, Shanxi and Jiangxi provinces through technical assistance and institutional capacity building. There are three components to the project. The first component of the project is Shandong energy efficiency scaling-up program, the second component of the project is Shanxi energy efficiency scaling-up program, and the third component of the project is Jiangxi energy efficiency scaling-up program. The activities to support under these components are: 1) developing energy efficiency service industry; 2) establishing an energy efficiency monitoring, supervision, and information management platform; 3) improving enterprise energy management system; 4) building capacity for energy efficiency program management and supervision in local enterprises and agencies in Shandong province; 5) strengthening the capacity of local enterprises and agencies in energy efficiency program management and supervision; and 6) developing energy pricing and fiscal policies to incentivize energy efficiency activities and investments.
