Projet : Resilient Infrastructure for Regional Economic Development and Job Creation - Salta Project
Identifiant : P510696
Pays : Argentina (Amérique latine et Caraïbes)
Secteur : Gouvernance
Coût total : 100.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 66 mois
Année d'approbation : 2026
Statut : en_cours

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
To enhance access to resilient infrastructure and increase institutional capacity in Salta to support economic development and job creation in mining and related sectors, and to act effectively in case of an eligible crisis or emergency.

Résumé :
The project aims to support the development of strategic infrastructure and logistics services in the Province of Salta through four main components: (1) Investment in resilient and safe road infrastructure;(2) Development of a logistics center to serve critical minerals and other productive sector firms;(3) Investment in water and wastewater treatment infrastructure for the local communities; (4) Support to the mining sector by strengthening sustainability, oversight, training, and transparency in mining operations.In addition, the project includes a CERC component and a component focused on Project Management.
