Projet : Emergency Infrastructure Rehabilitation and Living Conditions Improvement Project
Identifiant : P074006
Pays : Congo, Republic of (Afrique de l'Ouest et centrale)
Secteur : Transport
Coût total : 40.0 millions de dollars
Durée prévue : 57 mois
Année d'approbation : 2002
Statut : termine

Objectif de développement (PDO) :
The objectives of the project are to (a) reestablish basic economic activities and social services by rehabilitating primary, secondary and social infrastructure, (b) promote employment creation through labor intensive execution of public works, and (c) support institutional strengthening and capacity building to maintain primary, secondary, economic and social infrastructure.
